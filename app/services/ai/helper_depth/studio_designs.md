Studio designs in depth: every situation, size, layout, style, color theme, the editor's cards, the profile and "whose home" rules, lead tracking and downloads.

## Who gets Studio
- Premium, IDX and No-MLS Premium. Locked on Basic and No-MLS Basic.
- Luxe pieces (the **Luxe** layout, the **Luxe** color theme, and any **Luxe finish** metal) are IDX only. Other plans can open and preview them ("Luxe comes with IDX" / "Studio Luxe"), but can't create or keep them - pick another layout or theme, or choose **None** for the finish.
- No-MLS Premium: there's no MLS to pull from, so the property step shows "The home's details" - type the **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Price**, **Sqft** and add a **Photo** (JPG). "Not on the MLS? Type it in once, reuse it anytime."

## The Studio page (Tools > Studio)
- **Create a Design** starts the wizard. Or tap a situation under "What are you creating marketing for?" to jump straight in.
- **Fresh from the MLS** (MLS plans): your newest listings and latest sales that still need a post - one tap opens the right design (Coming Soon status -> Coming Soon, under contract/pending -> Under Contract, otherwise Just Listed; sales -> Just Sold). Tap the X ("Not now - don't suggest this one again") to dismiss one.
- Two tabs: **Your designs** and **Browse designs** (ideas, filterable by where it's posted, e.g. "Everywhere", "Feed posts (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn)").
- Your designs: search box ("Search designs or properties..."), status filter tabs **All, Draft, Ready, Exported, Published, Archived**. A draft becomes "Exported" once you download it.
- Each design card: **Edit**, **Copy** (makes a duplicate), **Download**, and a trash can ("Delete this design?" - "This removes it from your projects for good. This cannot be undone.").

## Step by step: the 9 wizard steps
Bar at the top shows each step; tap a finished step to go back. On a computer, Enter continues and the arrow keys flip options.

1. **Situation - "What are you marketing?"**
   - *Listing marketing (built from a property)*: **Just Listed**, **Coming Soon**, **Open House**, **Price Improvement**, **Under Contract**, **Just Sold**.
   - *Lead generation (no listing needed)*: **Home Valuation**, **Make Me Sell**, **Find Your Dream Home**, **Social Media Ad**.
2. **Where it's posted - "Where will it be posted?"** Grouped as Social media / Print & mail. Each shows its shape, exact size and file type (print = "PDF + PNG", social = "PNG"):
   - **Square Post** - 1:1, 1080 x 1080 px, Instagram & Facebook feed.
   - **Feed Post** - 4:5, 1080 x 1350 px, Instagram, Facebook & LinkedIn feed, and ads.
   - **Instagram Story** - 9:16, 1080 x 1920 px, Stories & Reels cover.
   - **Just Listed Flyer** / **Open House Flyer** - 8.5 x 11 in letter-size flyer.
   - **Postcard** - 6 x 4 in standard mailing size.
   - **Jumbo Postcard** - 9 x 6 in, "most used for farming".
   - **Letter Mailer** - 8.5 x 11 in page to mail or hand out.
   - **Door Hanger** - 4.25 x 11 in.
   Which sizes each situation offers:
   - Just Listed: Just Listed Flyer, Postcard, Jumbo Postcard, Square, Feed, Story.
   - Coming Soon, Price Improvement: Postcard, Jumbo, Letter Mailer, Square, Feed, Story.
   - Open House: Open House Flyer, Postcard, Jumbo, Door Hanger, Square, Feed, Story.
   - Under Contract: Postcard, Jumbo, Square, Feed, Story.
   - Just Sold: Postcard, Jumbo, Letter Mailer, Door Hanger, Square, Feed, Story.
   - Home Valuation, Make Me Sell: Postcard, Jumbo, Letter Mailer, Door Hanger, Square, Feed (no Story).
   - Find Your Dream Home: Postcard, Jumbo, Square, Feed, Story.
   - Social Media Ad: Feed, Square, Story only.
3. **Property - "Which property is this for?"** (listing situations)
   - Tabs depend on the situation (the real-estate advertising rule): Just Listed / Coming Soon / Price Improvement / Under Contract -> **My Listings** only. Just Sold -> **Recent Sales** only. Open House -> **My Listings** and **Approved to Host** (homes whose listing agent approved you to host).
   - **Skip - I'll fill in the details myself** continues without a property. When you skip (or on No-MLS plans), you must tick a box confirming you represent it, e.g. "I'm the listing agent for this home (or have the listing broker's written OK to advertise it)", "I represented the seller or the buyer in this sale", or "I'm the listing agent, or the listing agent approved me to host this open house."
   - **Lead photo**: tap any listing photo, or **Upload JPG**.
   - **Price on the design**: from the listing (Just Sold uses the sale price and shows "Sold for"; if no sale price, "Listed at"), **Enter a price**, or **No price - Leave it off** ("Turn it back on any time"). Price Improvement adds **Was (optional - shown struck through)**.
   - **Address on the design**: **Full address**, **Hide the house number**, or **Town only** ("For sellers who want privacy").
   - Open House only: **Open house date & time** - tap one of "Your scheduled open house" cards, or **Pick the date & time** / **A different date & time** (weekends highlighted, "Just the date" allowed). Optional.
   *Lead-gen situations instead show "Your message & photo"*: pick a ready headline or **Write your own** (up to 70 characters), then a photo from a built-in library of home exteriors or upload your own. Ready headlines:
   - Home Valuation: "What is your home worth today?", "Curious about your home's value?", "See what your home could sell for."
   - Make Me Sell: "What price would make you move?", "Would you sell if the price were right?", "Thinking about selling?"
   - Find Your Dream Home: "Find your dream home.", "Let's find your next home.", "Start your home search."
   - Social Media Ad: "Thinking about selling this year?", "Find out what your home is really worth.", "See new listings before anyone else.", "Ready to find your next home?"
4. **Layout - "Choose a layout"** (live previews). Ready to pick: **Keepsake** (instant photo print, taped down, handwritten headline), **Billboard** (giant poster type, photo as a card, facts on a ticker), **Slant** (diagonal color slab and round price sticker), **Postage** (the home as a stamp with a postmark, "Greetings from" your town), **Filmstrip** (three photos on a strip of film), and **Luxe** (framed in gold light on midnight - IDX). Every other layout (Cinematic, Horizon, Headline Band, Magazine Cover, Mosaic, Seal, Collage, Split, Statement, Gallery Frame, Arch, Swiss, and the QR mailers Ticket, Price Tag, Scan & Go, Bullseye, Neighbor Note, Scratch & Reveal) shows **Coming soon**.
5. **Style - "Choose a style"**: **Modern Luxury** is the one you can pick. Editorial, Minimal and Bold show Coming soon.
6. **Theme - "Choose a color theme"**: **Ivory** (white paper, antique-gold accents), **Stone** (warm greige, bronze), **Charcoal** (soft warm charcoal, champagne), **Forest** (deep muted green, brass), **Brand** (your Branding color as the background, with your logo), **Luxe** (midnight and gold - IDX). Below it, **Luxe finish**: **None** (theme accent), **Gold**, **Silver**, **Bronze** - the metals from your signature, applied to every accent. IDX only.
7. **Photo crop - "Frame your photo"**: drag and zoom, use **Quick framing**, or **Show the whole photo** ("Nothing cut off - the edges fill with a soft blur of it."). Your photo file is never cut.
8. **Leads - "Track the leads this brings in"** (see below).
9. **Finish - "Name it & create"**: **Design name**, a summary with **Change** links for every step, and **Your headshot** on/off ("On the design, beside your name" / "Off - just your name and contact"). Click **Create design**.
After creating: "Happy with this design?" - download right away (print: PDF "Exact size, 300 DPI - send to your printer" or PNG; social: **Save image** full resolution), **Open the editor**, or **Edit this design**. Social posts get "Your link" to paste in your caption, bio or Story.

## "A few details first" (the profile gate)
Real-estate ads must show who they're from, so Studio won't create or download a design until your profile has: **Your name**, **Your brokerage**, **Your headshot** (JPG), **Your mobile number** (10 digits), and for print pieces **Your brokerage's phone** (printed under your number). Fill them right there and tap **Save and continue**, or **Or edit everything in Settings**.

## Lead tracking and QR codes
- Pick **Which form?** (the **Best fit** is suggested for the situation: e.g. Home value for Home Valuation, Make me sell, Buyer form for Find Your Dream Home, How Can I Help for Social Ad). If you have no forms turned on: "Turn on a form".
- Mail pieces (postcards, jumbo, letter mailer, door hanger) and every lead-gen design are meant to bring in leads, so a form is expected. On other pieces you can choose **No thanks**; the QR then goes to the property page (open house: the open house sign-in; lead-gen: your Agent Hub; social: "No link needed").
- **Campaign**: **New campaign** (Recommended - "Its own link and QR - this piece's leads counted on their own", name up to 120 characters) or **Add to one I already have**. Then choose where people go after they submit: **My Agent Hub**, **My Sales & Listings**, **Stay on the thank-you screen**, or **My own link** (must be a full web address).
- Print pieces: **Put a QR code on it** on/off ("Off - you'll still get the link at the end"). Social posts never get a QR code - you get a link instead.

## The editor (Edit on any design)
Header: Undo (Ctrl+Z) / Redo (Ctrl+Shift+Z), zoom (50%-250%, tap the % to fit), **Download**, and for social posts **Matching Story** / **Matching Post** (makes the same design in the other size - Square/Feed <-> Story). "Everything saves automatically." Status reads Saving… / All changes saved / "Could not save - check your connection". Cards:
- **Photos** - swap the main photo ("Tap any listing photo to make it the main one"), **Add a photo**, **Adjust framing** (Zoom, Show the whole photo, Center/Bottom, Reset), **Extra photos** (tap the star to add; left empty, the next listing photos fill in). Keepsake has **Words under the photo** (handwritten on the white edge).
- **Words** - **Headline** with quick picks per situation (e.g. Just Listed / New Listing / Now Available / Welcome Home; Open House / Open This Weekend / Come See It; New Price / Price Improvement / Just Reduced; Under Contract / Pending / Offer Accepted; Just Sold / Another Happy Client / Closed; Coming Soon / Sneak Peek / Arriving Soon). Lead-gen pieces also have **Supporting line** and **Small label above**. **Street address** and **Town line**, address privacy (**Full address / No house # / Town only**), **Description** (**Use the listing's description** or **Remove the description**), **Highlights** (**Add a highlight**), and on social posts **Button on the post** (optional: Learn More, Contact Us, Send Message, Call Now, Get Quote, Sign Up, Book Now, Request Time, Get Directions, or No button).
- **Price, facts & open house** - price source (listing price or type your own), **Small label above the price** (Sold for, Listed at, Anticipated at, Offered at, Asking), **Leave the price off**, **+ Show a previous price, crossed out**, **Facts strip** (Show/Hide the facts), open house **Date / Starts / Ends**.
- **You** - **Show on this design** (Headshot); per-design **Name**, **Title** (e.g. REALTOR®), **Brokerage / team**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Website** ("Blank = whatever is on your profile. These changes only affect this one design."); **Different headshot** / **Use my profile photo**. **Logo**: **My logo**, **Team logo**, **Brokerage logo** or **No logo**, **Logo size** (Smaller / Normal / Larger). **Fine print**: blank uses your Brand Kit disclaimer or the standard Equal Housing line.
- **Look** - Layout, Style and Color theme (plus Luxe finish). "Same words and photos, arranged differently. Nothing you typed is lost when you switch."
- **Property** - **Attach a property** / **Change property**; **Refill from the listing** puts back the listing's own address, price, facts and description.
- **Leads & QR code** - change form or campaign, **QR code on this piece** on/off, **Where the QR code goes** (a campaign form, the open house sign-in, open house registration, My Agent Hub, My forms / Custom forms, or **A web address I type**), **Words beside the code** (e.g. "Scan for details"), **Stop tracking**.

## Downloading
- Print pieces: **Print-ready PDF** ("Exact size for your printer" - true size at 300 DPI) or **Image** / High-resolution image ("For email, texts or screens").
- Social posts: a full-resolution PNG at the exact size above.
- Reminder on the page: "Before it goes out: if your brokerage reviews marketing, get their OK first."

## Can't do / common mistakes
- Can't advertise another agent's listing or a sale you didn't represent - the picker only shows your homes (that's the MLS and REALTOR® rule).
- Can't pick Cinematic, Band, etc. or the Editorial/Minimal/Bold styles yet - Coming soon. Designs you already made with them still open and download as saved.
- Retired sizes (Facebook Post, LinkedIn Post, Email Graphic, Presentation Graphic, Just Sold Post, Mailer) can't be chosen for new designs; old ones still open, edit and export. The 4:5 Feed Post covers Facebook and LinkedIn.
- No Story size for Home Valuation or Make Me Sell.
- Download blocked? The profile gate is missing something (often the brokerage's phone for print).
- Brand theme looks wrong? It uses your Branding color - change it in Branding.
- Changing a campaign's after-submit page later affects QR codes already printed.
- Studio doesn't print or mail for you - download and send to your printer.
