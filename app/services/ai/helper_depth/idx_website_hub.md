# IDX WEBSITE (IDX plan only; top menu **IDX**, "Your site, your brand")

## Status and saving
- Status chip: **Live**, **Draft** or **Turned off**, with **Copy link** and **Open in new window**.
- Changes stay private until published. **Save draft** ("Saved - not published yet") vs **Publish changes** ("Your website is live."). Leaving with unsaved changes asks first (**Leave without saving** / **Keep editing**). Publishing can't be undone - there's no version history, so edit and publish again.
- If the site is switched off in Settings, the editor says it isn't shown to anyone.

## Pages the site has
- Home, About, Contact, Sold (when you have sales), Search Homes (map search), Featured Listings, Articles & Media (blog, videos and tours), Reviews (when on), one page per served town, home-value and property pages. Every home page has a tour request and question form.

## Editor tabs
- **Sections** ("Build Your Home Page"): drag to reorder, switch on/off, tap to edit, add from the catalogue - each arrives complete:
  - Live from the MLS: **Welcome Screen** (rotating MLS photos from your areas, your market line, a home search box and Buying / Selling / Home Value buttons from your premade forms), **Featured Homes** (from the Featured tab), **Just Listed** (your newest active listings), **Listing Spotlights** (published Spotlights), **Open Houses** (upcoming), **Recently Sold** (your closed sales), **Showcase Collection** (a button to one Showcase you choose, with your headline).
  - About you: **About Me** (photo, bio, track record, contact), **Client Reviews**, **Lead Form** (one of your forms filled in on the page; starts with How Can I Help), **Contact Band** (call/text/email).
  - Local: **Neighborhoods** (photo tiles for every served town with live for-sale counts and median prices).
  - Content: **Latest Articles**, Text, Photo, Divider, Heading.
- **Design** ("Look & Feel"): **Look comes from** **My Brand Kit** (follows Branding) or **Choose my own**. **Classic** ("Clean and bright... great for most agents") or **Luxe** ("Cinematic: deep panels, metallic accents, gliding photo strips"; gold, silver or bronze). Any accent color, **Ivory or Midnight** default, **Let visitors switch** (sun/moon button), Heading font and Body font (Elegant serif / Modern sans), logo (or your name), **Loading mark** (your logo or **Use my initials** spins while pages load), and **My email signature in the footer**.
- **Featured** ("Featured Homes"): the header homes - newest homes for sale that match your towns, price and home type, with **My own listings first**. The Featured page can show your listings, your areas, a custom collection, or a mix.
- **Media** ("Videos & Tours"): **Show Videos**, **Show House Tours** - they come from your Agent Hub's Media tab.
- **Reviews**: **Show reviews on my website** (a Reviews page with your rating, a featured review and every review, plus menu and footer links; joins the menu once you have a review) and **Only show 4 and 5 stars** (also applies to your Agent Hub).
- **Areas** ("Areas You Serve"): see town pages below.
- **Google** ("How You Look on Google"): Site name ("Your name on Google"), **Tab icon** (Use my logo / Upload my own / house icon), **When your link is shared** picture (auto-made from your photo, name and towns until you upload one - about 1200 x 630), **Your main result** headline and description, **Sub-link descriptions**, **Let Google find my site** (off hides the whole site from search), **Share my star rating with Google** (only sent with 3+ reviews averaging 4.0+, Reviews page on, and every review counted - not the 4-5 star filter). After publishing, OpenHousePal tells Google for you; new sites usually appear within a few days to two weeks. Optional: **Use my own Google account too** (Search Console verification tag).
- **Domain** ("Your Domain"): below.

## Town pages (Areas)
- Add a **City / Town** or **Market Area / Neighborhood** with its 2-letter state (e.g. Villanova, PA; Rittenhouse Square, PA) → **Add this area**. New ones show **Researching...** for a while.
- Up to 15. Arrows set the order (main town first). Removing asks "Stop serving this area?" - its page leaves your site.
- Each page: Overview, Homes (live listings with price filters and sort), Market, Schools (elementary, middle, high, private), Living here (grocery, parks, restaurants, shopping, town centers, medical), Getting around (commute, transit), FAQ - sections only show when there's real data.
- Every plan sets "Towns you serve" in Account setup (used by forms, buyer matching and blog town cards); only IDX turns them into pages.

## Pop-up forms and lead capture
- Every lead button (What's my home worth?, Start my home search, tour requests) opens your real form in a window over the page; answers land in Leads. Edit forms in Tools → Forms & Invites.
- Visitors can **Save this search** / **Create my free Showcase** - a private Showcase emailed to them, landing in your Leads.
- Google sign-in ("Continue as...") on the site gives you the visitor's name, email and the home they viewed (Website sign-in follow-up).

## Blog on the site
- Published posts appear on the Blog page; add **Latest Articles** to the home page. Per article, **Show on my IDX Site** controls it.

## Your domain
- Free address set up automatically: yourname.openhousepal.com ("Always available, even if a custom domain has an issue").
- **I already have one**: connect a domain you own. The easy way: some domain companies (GoDaddy, Cloudflare...) can do it for you in one click; or follow the step-by-step guide for your company (GoDaddy, Squarespace, Namecheap, Wix, Cloudflare, Network Solutions, Porkbun, others) - point "www" to your new website and forward the bare domain. Status: Waiting for DNS → We verify it → **Setting up HTTPS...** → HTTPS live (or "Problem — check DNS"). New domains usually open within an hour, sometimes up to a day. You can add more domains; the one marked Default is the address shared.
- **Get a new one**: search a name, see the price ("renews yearly"), enter owner name and address → registered in your name at Name.com with your home address kept private, set up for you with https. **Stop renewing** or move it any time (get a transfer code first). If the purchase fails, the payment is refunded.
- Either option may show "Connecting your own domain opens soon" / "Buying a domain here opens soon" if it isn't switched on for the account yet.
- **Disconnect domain** warns: the site goes back to the free address, new links use openhousepal.com, and links/QR codes you handed out with the domain stop opening.
- When a domain with an old website is connected, OpenHousePal checks for pages from the old site so old links can forward.

## On/off and which links your QR codes use (Settings → IDX Website)
- **IDX Website** switch: On ("your IDX website is live on your addresses") / Off ("you're using a website of your own"; asks **Turn off your IDX website?** - "Nothing is deleted"). Off: the site isn't shown and nothing links to it; your Agent Hub, open houses and leads keep working. Back on = exactly as published.
- **Use my own domain for my links** (once your own domain is live): new sign-in QR codes, Guest Passes, Showcases, forms, Agent Hub and signature links use your domain. Off keeps them on openhousepal.com. Links you already handed out keep working either way.
- Also there: Your Website status (Published & live / Draft — not published yet / Turned off — not visible to anyone), **View My Website**, **Edit My Website**, **More website settings**.

# AGENT HUB (Premium, IDX, No-MLS Premium; Tools → Agent Hub, "One link, all of you")

## Tabs (with a live preview; **Views** and **Interacted** counts on top)
- **Profile**: photo (drag the circle to reposition; JPG under 5MB), Agent name, Professional title, Brokerage name, Team name ("Only if you are on a team"), Market or service area, About me, RE License number (private; locked once saved - support changes it). Shared with Settings → Profile.
- **Contact**: Call, Text and Email buttons; Social Links (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Website); **Contact Card** - what people save with **Save Contact**: name always, plus Mobile Phone, Email, Brokerage, Photo, Website, Agent Hub link, Job Title, Office Phone, Office Address, Team, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Custom Link and a short note. Turning one off only leaves it off the saved card.
- **Listings & Sales**:
  - My Listings: Bright plans pull active, pending and coming-soon listings (**Refresh Listings**); choose which show. **Manually Add** → **Add Listing** (Street Address, City, State, ZIP, Price, Status Active / Coming Soon / Under Contract, photo). Sort, **Carousel view**, **Auto scroll**.
  - Recent Sales: found by your RE License number (**Refresh Sales**); choose which show - they also rotate through your email signature. **Manually Add** → **Add Sold Property** (address, Price, Date Closed, photo). Sort Newest / Price High / Price Low, **Min price to display**, **Sold-price stats**, **Carousel view**, **Auto scroll** (Luxe only). No-MLS Premium adds every listing and sale by hand; sales also print on the Brag Sheet.
- **Media**: Videos (YouTube channel link with **Auto-update from channel** / **Load Recent Videos**, or individual links; favorite = always first), House Tours (playlist link or individual links), Blogs (Studio articles auto-synced, plus manual links).
- **Layout**: **Agent Hub theme** (My Brand Kit or Choose my own: **Classic color** or **Luxe finish** - Luxe is visible to visitors on IDX only; others see "IDX · Preview"); **Ivory or Midnight** (also your Sales page); **Profile header** (Centered - "Photo on top, everything centered" / Side by side - "Photo beside your name and details, stats below"); **Reviews** (Show reviews on my Agent Hub, Only show 4 and 5 star reviews, One big card / Two cards, Move on their own (phones)); **Agent Hub Layout** (drag My Listings, Recent Sales, Videos, House Tours, Blogs, Reviews into order; switch each on/off - empty sections stay off); **Star Rating** (rating and review button on your profile card). Changes go live right away.
- **Link**: **Public URL** → **Custom URL ending** (letters, numbers, hyphens) → **Save URL**; **Copy URL**, **Open Public Page**, **Share URL**.

## Changing your hub address
- Confirmation **Change your Agent Hub link?**: "Pick a name you'll keep." Your current link keeps working after the change (cards, signature, social bios, printed QR codes - your hub, forms and blog all still open), and no other agent can take it. But only your most recent old link keeps working: change it again later and the one before stops working and is freed up. The new link becomes your main one.
- Hub pages: openhousepal.com/your-name, /sales (Past Sales and Current Listings), listings, blogs, and /review. On IDX with your own domain connected, the hub also opens on your domain.

## Where listings link to
- Settings → Profile → **Where Your Listings Link To**: each listing on your hub and signature opens its Spotlight (if published) or the simple Listing Page ("Presented by" you, with showing and question forms). You can send one back to the plain page.

## Can't do
- Basic and No-MLS Basic don't have a hub. Luxe isn't public below IDX. The hub shows "This link isn't active" when the plan lapses or no longer includes it.

# REVIEW HUB (Premium, IDX, No-MLS Premium; Tools → Reviews, "Five stars, on repeat")
- Needs your Agent Hub set up first.
- **Your review link** (yourname/review): **Copy link**, **Text it**, **Email it**, **See what clients see**. "Send it after every closing."
- How it works: clients pick stars and write a few words. 4-5 stars go public and their text copies automatically, then **Post on Google** / **Post on Zillow** - one paste and done. 1-3 stars come to you as **Private feedback**, never posted, and create an Unhappy review follow-up right away (switchable on the Follow-ups page).
- Settings: **Show on my Agent Hub**, **Show on my IDX website** (IDX plan), **Only show 4 and 5 stars** (lower ones stay saved privately).
- **Where happy clients post**: paste your Google link (Business Profile → Ask for reviews → copy the short link) and Zillow link (the link behind **Write a review** on your Zillow profile - not the profile link) → **Save links**.
- **Bring in your Google reviews**: paste your Google Place ID (starts with ChIJ…) - Google shares up to 5.
- **Bring in reviews from other sites**: **Paste a bunch** (Zillow, Google, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Facebook, somewhere else → **Find the reviews**; uses a little AI allowance; copied word for word with a "via" label and original date; you confirm they're real) or **Add one by hand**.
- Received list: Average rating, Shown publicly, Posted to Google / Zillow; each review **Hide** / **Show** or **Delete permanently**.
