Listing Spotlight in depth: making a page, all 9 wizard steps and their options, sections, open houses, files, agents, publishing, the page's address, stats and the leads it brings in.

## Who gets it
- **IDX only.** Shows locked on Basic and Premium; hidden on No-MLS plans.
- Only your own current listings (you're the listing or co-listing agent). Not sold homes, not other agents' listings, no typed-in addresses.

## The Spotlight list (Tools > Listing Spotlight)
- Top stats: **Live pages**, **Visitors** ("Unique visitors - distinct browsers, your own visits excluded"), **Leads**.
- **New Listing Spotlight** -> "Which home gets the spotlight?" -> **Start building**. If that home already has one: "This home already has a Spotlight page - you can still make another." **Ready for a Spotlight** lists your listings without a page.
- Each card: a **Live / Offline / Draft** badge ("Not published yet", "Offline - visitors can't open it right now"), listing status, and **Visitors**, **Visits** ("Total visits") and **Leads** ("Leads from this page").
  - Live: **Open**, **QR** ("Scan to open your Listing Spotlight"), **Download** (the QR), **Share link**, **Unpublish**, trash can.
  - Not live: **Edit**, **Preview**, **Publish** (or **Finish** if never published), **Delete page**.
- **Unpublish / Take offline**: "Visitors and your QR codes won't open it until you publish it again. Nothing is deleted - tap Publish on the card any time." Republishing says "Your page is live again."
- **Delete** ("Delete this property website?") is permanent.

## First open: automatic setup
"Setting up your site…" runs **Picking the standout amenities**, **Writing the highlights**, **Polishing the property story** and **Writing about the town** ("A few seconds - everything stays editable"). Uses a small amount of your monthly AI. If the AI writer is down: "The AI writer isn't available right now - please try again in a little while."

## The 9 steps
1. **Design - "Pick a Design"**: **Luxe** ("Midnight and gold - full-bleed, film-like, the OpenHousePal Luxe look") or **Editorial Estate** ("Paper-light, like a magazine feature"). **Select**; **Preview** shows a sample. Same content either way - switch any time.
2. **Header - "The Header"**
   - **Main photo**: "Tap any photo to make it your header."
   - Type: **Still photo** ("Your main photo with a slow, gentle zoom"), **Photo slideshow** ("Your listing photos dissolve one into the next" - starts with your main photo, then the next photos in listing order, about 6 seconds each), **Living photo** - shows **Coming soon**.
   - **How it moves**: **Slow zoom out**, **Slow zoom in**, **Both (drifts in and out)**, **No movement**.
   - **Frame your photo** (required): set the center on the **Computer** tab AND the **Phone** tab - drag the box so the cross sits on the home, then **Use this spot** (or **Center it**). Each tab shows "Needs you" until set. **How much it zooms**: **Subtle**, **Medium**, **Strong** (both screens). The Bright MLS badge is kept off screen automatically.
   - You can't continue or publish until both screens are set: "One thing first: set the center of your header photo for both a computer and a phone."
3. **Story - "The Story"**: **Short & polished** (AI rewrite of your listing description - "nothing made up") or **Original description**. Edit the words directly; **Write it again** makes a new short version.
   - Switches: **Monthly payment calculator** ("Buyers see an estimated monthly payment and can play with the numbers") and **Follow this home & fact sheet** ("Guests get an email from you when the price drops, an open house is set, or it goes under contract or sells - with your photo and number. They can also print the key facts.").
   - **Photo beside the story**: "Picked by AI" (it looked at every photo), or **Choose a different photo** ("Your pick").
4. **Stand-outs - "What Stands Out"**
   - **Featured amenities**: pre-picked from the MLS, big-ticket items first. Tap to add or remove, up to 12. ("No MLS feature data for this listing" if none.)
   - **Highlights** - the phrases that scroll across the page (e.g. "Curved Hanging Staircase"), up to 8. AI can suggest more from the description.
   - **Notable facts** - optional, up to 3 (e.g. "45,000-Gallon Pool").
5. **The town - "About [town]"**: "Life in [town]", written for you, with a free credited photo (Wikimedia Commons - the photographer is credited on your page). Edit any line: **One line about the town**, **Known for**, **Downtown** (name, where it is, what's there), **Why people move here**, **Getting around** (destination or line + travel time), **The outdoors**, **New in town (optional)**. **Write it again** / **Write it for me**, **Change photo** / **Pick a photo** / your own, or no photo. **Shown on the page** / **Hidden on the page**. "Written from general knowledge - give it a quick read."
6. **Rooms - "Room by Room"** (optional): "Walk buyers through the home, room by room." **Find my home's best spaces** - AI picks the spaces that sell it (each gets its own full-screen moment with photos and a few lines). It shows the cost first (e.g. "Under a cent of your monthly AI"). If unsure, it asks you a quick question instead of guessing. Or set it up by hand, or **Skip it**.
   - Room types: Exterior / Arrival, Living Room / Great Room, Kitchen, Dining Room, Primary Suite, Additional Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Grounds / Backyard, Pool & Spa, Porch / Patio, Garage / Automotive, Office / Den, Basement / Rec Room, Custom.
   - Open a room to rename it, pick its photos, edit **Section copy**, hide or remove it. Every room you keep needs its check tapped ("Tap when this room looks right") before you can move on.
7. **Extras - "Video, Tour & Files"** (all optional; empty items don't show)
   - **AI video tour**: pick one of your Media Lab videos of this home, or **None**. **Make a full AI video tour of this home** opens Media Lab in a new tab with the photos ready.
   - **3D tour**: paste a Matterport (or any 3D tour) link - must start with https://.
   - **Floor plans**: JPG or PNG, up to 12MB each.
   - **Documents**: brochure, disclosures, HOA docs - PDFs (25MB) or images (15MB), up to 10. Or **Add a link instead**. Tap a name to rename it ("that's what visitors see").
   - **Open Houses**: pulled in automatically from Bright MLS and your Open House Manager; each drops off once it's over. **Add a date yourself** (weekends in gold, crossed-out days already have one) - it then also shows in Open House Manager, your dashboard and on Discovery for other agents. Edit or remove ones you added.
   - **Agents**: you're shown from your profile ("You - merged into your profile card"); you can add a co-agent (Full name, Title, Phone, Email, Brokerage, License #, headshot).
8. **Sections - "Show or Hide Sections"**: switches with a live preview - Property Story, Photo Gallery, Features & Amenities, AI Video, 3D Virtual Tour, Floor Plans, Property Details, Location & Schools, Explore Nearby (places), Payment Estimator, Open Houses, Documents, Listing Agent, About the Town, Follow this home & fact sheet, My email signature card (in Listing Agent - off unless you turn it on). Sections with nothing in them never show.
9. **Review & publish**: checklist (**Amenities**, **Highlights**, **Story**, **Town**), a card for every step (tap to change), **Preview my page**, **Publish my page**.

## Saving and publishing
- Autosaves: "Saving…", "Unsaved", "All changes saved". Once live, edits go live by themselves ("Updating your live page…" -> "Saved & live"; status "Live · up to date").
- Publishing while AI is still writing: "Your site will publish once these are done - you can leave this page, it finishes on its own."
- After publishing: **Copy**, **QR**, **Text**, **Email**, **Open**.

## The page's address
- Every page gets its own address: the home's street as a sub-address of openhousepal.com (e.g. 26-old-gulph-rd.openhousepal.com).
- If you've connected your own domain to your IDX website, the page is also at yourdomain.com/listings/[home], and the share link uses that.
- Listings on your Agent Hub and in your email signature open your Spotlight once it's published (Settings > Profile > Where Your Listings Link To).

## What visitors can do (and where it goes)
- **Schedule a tour** (In person or Video tour, preferred day and time, a note) and **Request info** / ask a question (topics: Is it still available?, Price & offers, Taxes & HOA, Condition & updates, Disclosures, Something else).
- Request documents (emailed to them), follow the home, print the fact sheet, play with the payment estimator.
- Tour requests, questions and document requests land in your **Leads**. You also get the **Spotlight weekly report** email on Mondays (if on in Tools > Email).

## Can't do / common mistakes
- Stuck on Header: set both Computer and Phone. Stuck on Rooms: tick every room's check, or remove the room.
- Living photo isn't available yet.
- A 3D link without https:// won't show.
- Deleting can't be undone - use Unpublish.
- The town section is written from general knowledge - check it before publishing.
- Only one design (Luxe or Editorial Estate) per page at a time, but you can switch any time.
