Open House Kits in depth: building a kit (MLS and No-MLS), every document and its options, every switch, what guests get (sign-in, Guest Pass, disclosure, second visit), Kiosk and Clipboard, undated kits and hosting dates, hosting another agent's listing, and insights per hosting day.

## Who gets what
- Every plan can make kits with QR sign-in, Kiosk, Clipboard paper sheet and Brag Sheet.
- Basic, Premium, IDX: home picked from Bright MLS, plus Similar Homes, Cheat Sheet, Guest Pass, Disclosure & Second Visits, Discovery and Manager.
- Premium, IDX: Guest Showcases. IDX: **+ Spotlight** on your own listing.
- No-MLS Basic / No-MLS Premium: you type the home in. No Similar Homes, Cheat Sheet, Guest Pass, Discovery or Manager. No-MLS Premium gets the **Opens Hub / Opens Nothing** switch.

## Kits are undated and reusable
- One kit per home, used for every open house you hold there - no reprinting. A kit has no date of its own.
- Hosting dates come from elsewhere: approved host requests (Discovery), team asks you claimed, and opens posted in Bright MLS. Approved hosting dates go on your connected calendar with the home's address, the listing agent's host notes and a link to your kit.
- The QR code keeps working every time. If the same person signs in twice within a few hours, it counts once.

## Building a kit - MLS plans (Opens → My Opens)
1. Type in **Enter property address...**, pick the home from the suggestions, tap **Generate Kit**. (You must pick a suggestion; a home with no photos can't make a kit.)
2. **Pick similar homes** ("Similar Homes Sheet"): **Refine Your Search** with Min/Max Price, **Min Beds**, **Min Baths**, **Min Acres** and the search radius in miles ("Search area centered on this home"). Results update as you change filters; tap homes to add them. Each card can show "Same school". If you're the listing or co-listing agent, Similar Homes starts OFF ("guests stay focused on your listing") - tap **Pick similar homes instead** to include it. **Include Similar Homes** puts it back.
3. **Choose the cover photo** ("Select Cover Image for Sign-in Sheet"): tap a photo, then drag the preview to frame it. The preview mirrors the printed Sign-In Sheet with "Scan to Sign In".
4. **Cheat Sheet photos** ("Select Photos for the Open House Cheat Sheet"): pick up to 3 photos, drag inside each frame to center it. The button shows the Recap cost, e.g. "Continue: AI Recap - under 1% of your monthly AI", or "Continue uses no AI" when Recap is off.
5. **Review your kit** ("Your kit" - "Tap a document to see it"): each document with its own chips and a live preview:
   - Sign-In Flyer: **Brand** (your photo, name and brokerage in a pill across the photo) and **Ivory / Midnight** (the on-screen sign-in theme only - "Doesn't change the printed flyer").
   - Similar Homes: **Match** (% Match badges, best match first) and **Include**.
   - Brag Sheet: **Sold Stats** (homes sold, total volume, average price).
   - Cheat Sheet: **Recap** (swaps the long MLS description for a short AI "Highlights of..." paragraph, 3 printed lines max, written when the kit is made).
   - Your choices are remembered for your next kit. Tap **Looks good - next**.
6. **After a guest signs in**: set **Guest Pass**, **Guest Showcases** and **Follow-ups for this open house** (On, or Off - "No follow-ups from this open house - handy for a busy one. Guests still land in Leads"). With a calendar connected you also choose **Put them on my calendar** or **Keep off my calendar**. Tap **Save & Finish**.

## Building a kit - No-MLS plans
- "Type the home's address, add its details and photos, and get a QR sign-in sheet and flyer in a minute." Tap **Generate Kit** ("No Bright MLS needed").
- **Home details**: Street address, City, State, ZIP, Price, Square feet, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, and photos (JPG or PNG, pick several at once; at least one). **Make cover** marks the first photo. Wait for uploads to finish.
- Then cover photo, review, after-sign-in choices, **Save & Finish**.
- Your Brag Sheet needs a sale: if you have none, the kit asks "Your Brag Sheet needs a sale first" → **Add a sale** (it also goes on your Recent Sales list).

## My Opens page
- Top: **Find Open Houses** ("Discover brokerage listings near you", MLS plans) and **Manage hosts & view insights** (Manager).
- **Your Opens**: **Search your opens...**, totals for **Guests**, **Completion** and **Unrepresented**.
- Each card: **Visitors**, **Kit** (Documents), **Kiosk**, **Notes** (on an open you're hosting for someone else: **Notes & Info**, which also shows the listing agent's host notes for that open), trash icon, and the switches.

## Documents (the Kit button) and their buttons
- **Sign In** (Branded QR Sign in Sheet): **Brand**, **Ivory/Midnight**, **Refresh** (pulls the latest price onto the flyer), and **Clipboard**.
- **Clipboard**: a printable pencil-and-paper sign-in sheet with the same questions as the QR sign-in (name, email, phone, "Have an agent? (active agreement)" Yes/No). One-tap **Print**. Paper sign-ins are not typed into the app for you - ask guests to scan the QR so they land in Leads.
- **Similar Homes** (MLS plans): **Choose Homes / Edit** ("Pick different similar listings to show"), **Match %**, **On / Off** ("Off for this open house" + Include), **Refresh** (updates prices and removes homes that sold or left the market; "Similar homes are already up to date" if nothing changed).
- **Brag Sheet** ("Agent Brag Sheet"): bio and recent sales. **Sold Stats**, **Resync Sales** ("Recent sales resynced from your Agent Hub"). On Premium and IDX with Showcases and Similar Homes on, it adds a Showcase pitch page with a "Setup My Own Showcase" QR.
- **Cheat Sheet** (MLS-linked home only): facts, description, schools and "What's Nearby". **Edit** (photos), **Recap**, **Refresh Data** (re-pulls price, beds/baths/sqft and description; if the description changed and Recap is on, the Recap is rewritten - one AI call). The Cheat Sheet has a QR for **Request Seller Disclosure** and **Schedule a Second Visit**.
- Every preview: **Print** (straight to the printer) or download the print-ready PDF.

## The switches on each kit
- **Guest Showcases** (Premium, IDX): ON - everyone without an agent gets their own Showcase of similar homes and a Showcase button on their Guest Pass. Starts OFF on your own listing. Turning it off opens **Turn off Showcases?**: choose what guests still get - **Similar homes** on the Guest Pass and/or a **"Get my Showcase"** button (one is built only if they tap it) - then **Turn off Showcases** or **Keep them on**. Off also removes the Brag Sheet pitch page. Someone who scans "See more homes like these" on a printed Similar Homes sheet still gets a Showcase.
- **Guest Pass** (Basic, Premium, IDX; on by default): ON - right after signing in the guest's phone opens their Guest Pass. OFF - they just see "Thanks for signing in!". Never shown on a Kiosk tablet.
- **Similar Homes**: on/off from Documents or the kit. Off = no sheet and no other homes on the Guest Pass; your picks are kept for when you turn it back on.
- **Opens Hub / Opens Nothing** (No-MLS Premium): after sign-in, open your Agent Hub, or just the thank-you screen. Shows "Agent Hub (finish setting it up first)" until your hub is ready.
- **+ Spotlight / Finish Spotlight** (IDX, your own listing): make a Listing Spotlight; once published it's featured at the top of every Guest Pass.
- Turning Showcases on later doesn't build Showcases for guests who already signed in - use the per-visitor Showcase switch.
- The follow-ups choice is set only while making the kit; afterwards use Leads → Follow-ups (master switch or situation switches) or a lead's Alert settings.

## What guests see
- Sign-in: **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, then "Do you have an active agreement with a real estate agent?" - tapping an answer signs them in. Everyone lands in Leads; guests without an agent get an automatic "reach out" follow-up (unless off).
- **Guest Pass** (link works for 60 days): photos, "Take the full tour", the neighborhood, **Similar Homes · Live** (today's prices, price drops, under contract/coming soon tags; sold homes drop off), **Cheat Sheet** ("Schools, taxes & nearby"), one-tap **Seller Disclosure**, **Second Tour** (pick up to 2 day + morning/afternoon/evening options), their Showcase card or **Create my Showcase**, "Ask [agent] a quick question", and a host card (Call, Text, Email, Hub, Website).
- Second-visit page (from the Cheat Sheet QR): "Pick a few times to come back for another look", then asks whether they'd like the seller's disclosure too (**Yes please / No thanks**).
- Showcase invite email to guests without an agent goes out 1 hour after sign-in by default; change it in Tools → Email → "Showcase invite after your open house" → **Goes out** (Right away, 15 or 30 minutes after, 1 hour after).

## Visitors window
- Counts: **Visitors**, **No agent**, **Has agent**; everyone grouped by day with email and phone.
- **Star** (asks **Star Showcase too** or **Just the lead**), **Notes** (shared with their lead), **Activity** ("Everything they did on their Guest Pass" - e.g. Looked through the Similar Homes, Opened the Cheat Sheet, Asked for the seller disclosure, time on it, came back).
- **Showcase for this visitor** (Premium/IDX, guests without an agent): ON builds their Showcase and schedules the invite; OFF cancels a waiting invite and turns their Showcase off. If the invite email is switched off in Tools → Email, no invite is sent.

## Disclosure & Second Visits (MLS plans)
- Requests from the Cheat Sheet QR or Guest Pass sit at the top of My Opens while waiting. Tabs **Upcoming** / **Past**. Each shows the source (Guest Pass or Cheat Sheet QR) and "Also wants disclosure" when asked.
- **Mark sent** / **Mark scheduled** moves it to Past and checks off its follow-up. It does NOT send anything - you send the disclosure or book the visit yourself.

## Kiosk
- **Kiosk** on a card → **Choose Sign-In Mode**: **Sign-In Kiosk** (form on the tablet, resets after each guest) or **Sign-In Sheet** (no printer needed: rotating photos plus a QR guests scan with their own phone). Press and hold the exit button to leave.

## Hosting another agent's listing (MLS plans)
- **Discovery** (Opens → Discovery): set your area (**Enter address or landmark** + miles) and filters (Brokerages, Exclude/Only Agents, Cities, Districts, Min Bedrooms/Bathrooms/Sqft/Price; **Reset All**). Saved automatically.
- **Request to Host** (or tap an empty weekend day): pick one day, or two to offer a second open → time → note → **Send request** / **Send 2 requests**. If the listing agent isn't on OpenHousePal you get an **Example Outreach Email** to send yourself.
- Refusals: the day passed, under contract/off market, coming soon before it goes active ("Hosting once it's active"), that day already booked, you already host another open that day (one per day), or "Not Accepting Host Requests" (team-only or a different brokerage).
- **My Requests**: Sent Requests, Team Requests (one tap to say yes - first to claim wins), Team Invites, Declined / Cancelled. **Accept New Time**, **Decline**, **Other time**, **Withdraw request** / **Can't host anymore?**. Each side can suggest another time only once; then it's yes or no.
- Once approved it shows under "You're confirmed to host" on My Opens: **Open kit** ("Kit ready - use the same one") or **Build kit**. Guests who sign in on your kit are your leads.

## Your own listings (Manager, MLS plans)
- Listings where you're the MLS listing agent appear automatically (**Refresh Listings**). Counts: Scheduled, Needs host, Avg guests, Priority.
- **Host Requests**: **Approve** (tells the host, books it, declines other requests for the same day), **Decline**, **Other time**.
- **Manage Hosts**: Find a Host (a day or **Find a host for any day** → time incl. Any time → **Ask my whole team**, **Or one teammate**, **Hosted for you before**, or search any agent by **Name, email or MLS ID** → note), **Scheduled Opens** (**Cancel this open house** notifies the host), **Host Notes** (lockbox, parking, alarm, seller wishes - hosts see them before their open), **Who has hosted here**.
- **My Team**: add trusted agents (they accept an invite). **Who can ask to host my listings**: **Only my team** (everyone else sees "Not accepting host requests") or **My team and agents at my brokerage** (the usual choice).

## Insights per hosting day
- **View Insights** ("Open House Insights"): **Opens hosted**, guests, **had no agent**, **Want similar homes**; **Coming up** (confirmed hosts, plus opens "Posted in Bright MLS") and **Past opens**.
- Results are counted per hosted day, not per kit: each approved hosted day shows the host's sign-ins at their kit for that home on that day only (sign-ins on other days don't count). Your own kit adds one row for each day that drew sign-ins.
- Guest names stay with the host; the listing agent sees the counts.

## Can't do / common mistakes
- Removing a kit: trash → **Remove Listing**. Visitors, leads and notes are kept and QR codes keep working, but the card and PDFs leave My Opens. No undo - make a new kit.
- Ivory/Midnight never changes the printout.
- MLS plans can't type in a home by hand; No-MLS kits never get Similar Homes or a Cheat Sheet.
- There's no button to type paper (Clipboard) sign-ins into Leads.
- Can't mark a disclosure request as not done again once marked.
