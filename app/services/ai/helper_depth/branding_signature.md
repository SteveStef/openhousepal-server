Branding and the Email Signature in depth: your look (Classic/Luxe, Ivory/Midnight, metals), brand color and "use it everywhere", logos, fonts, print background and disclaimer; then every signature section, the banner and stats, buttons, looks, smart links, installing in each email app and when to re-copy.

## Branding (Brand Kit)
**Who**: every plan. The **Luxe** look is IDX only ("Included with the IDX plan") - others can preview it.
**Where**: the Branding banner at the bottom of the Tools page (phone menu: **Branding**). Page title "Your brand, everywhere". "Tap to choose - everything saves on its own, no Save button needed."

### Your look
- **Look**: **Classic** ("Clean and bright, in your color") or **Luxe** ("Metal shine, serif type, cinematic light" - IDX).
- **Finish** (Luxe): **Gold**, **Silver** or **Bronze**.
- **Color** (Classic): pick a swatch, **Custom color** / **Any color**.
- **Ivory or Midnight**: bright pages or deep, dark pages.
- The intro line changes by plan, e.g. "Pick your color once - your Agent Hub, website, forms, signature, invites, Spotlight and designs all wear it." (IDX).

### Where it shows ("Your features at a glance")
Each feature shows a status: **Uses your brand**, **Its own look**, **Not set up yet**, **With the IDX plan**, **No forms yet**, **New invites start in it**, **Always your brand**, **Pick "Brand" in a design**.
- **Agent Hub** - follows your brand while its "Look comes from" is **My Brand Kit**.
- **IDX website** - follows it while its Theme is on My Brand Kit (no republish needed).
- **Forms** - every form set to My Brand Kit changes with it.
- **Signature** - follows it while on My Brand Kit. "Already installed? Copy it into your email again after a change."
- **Invites** - new invitations start in your brand color; live ones made in it change with it.
- **Spotlight** - always uses your brand color.
- **Studio designs** - only designs set to the **Brand** color theme.
- **Use this color for all features** -> "Use it everywhere?": switches every feature back to My Brand Kit, replacing their own color choices. "You can still give any one of them its own look later, in that feature."
- **See it everywhere** - a preview of every feature in your color.

### Logos, fonts & print
- **Logos**: three slots - **Personal**, **Team**, **Brokerage**. "JPG or PNG. Used on your designs, Spotlight and signature." Up to 10MB. **Upload** / **Replace** / remove. In a Studio design you pick which one shows (or none) and its size.
- **Print background**: "The background color on your flyers, social posts, CMA and Spotlight. Your brand color comes from Your look above."
- **Fonts**: **Heading font** and **Body font** - **Editorial Serif**, **Modern Sans**, **Structured Mono**.
- **Default Disclaimer**: "Shown on print materials where required. Leave blank to omit." (e.g. "Equal Housing Opportunity. Information deemed reliable but not guaranteed."). Studio designs use it as their fine print unless you type a per-design one.

## Email Signature
**Who**: IDX and No-MLS Premium. Locked on Basic, Premium and No-MLS Basic. No-MLS Premium shows the listings and sales you enter by hand; IDX syncs them from the MLS. The Luxe signature theme is IDX only.
**Where**: Tools > Signature. Autosaves ("Saving..." / "Saved"). Five sections: **Identity**, **Social**, **Buttons**, **Banner**, **Customize**.

### Identity
"Headshot, name, title, brokerage, team, phone & email come from your Profile in Settings." Switch each on/off: **Headshot**, **Name**, **Job Title**, **Brokerage**, **Team** (needs a team name in Settings), **Phone**, **Email**, **Agent Hub link**. **Verified badge**: "Small blue checkmark next to your name - a design accent, not a real verification." Drag your photo to fix a tight crop.

### Social
Toggle which links show: **Website**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **YouTube**, **TikTok**. Links come from Social Links in Settings - "A link with nothing filled in can't be turned on." No LinkedIn. Icons pop in gently, then hold.

### Buttons
"My Agent Hub" always leads, plus up to 2 more buttons linked to your real forms: **Ready to Buy or Sell?**, **What's My Home Worth?**, **Schedule a Showing**, **I'm Buying**, **I'm Selling**, **Make Me Sell**. Each opens that form exactly like on your Agent Hub and always points at its current version. Switch each on/off or remove it. New signatures start with Ready to Buy or Sell? and What's My Home Worth? when you have those forms.

### Banner ("Show my listings & sales")
- **Featured listing** - "Most recent listings, photo + price + specs".
- **Recent sales** - "One or two show like a featured listing (two take turns); three or more show side by side."
- Smart: shows your active listing when you have one, and falls back to recent sales when you don't. "Automatically rotates to a different listing/sale each day."
- **My stats** - Volume / Closed / Avg Price, on the right edge of the card or as a slim strip under your name.
- "Listings & sales sync from the MLS - manage them in Settings."

### Customize
- **Look** - "Where the look comes from": **My Brand Kit** (follows Branding) or **Choose my own** ("Pick a color, Luxe and Ivory or Midnight below").
- **Premade theme - Luxe**: gold, serif and the Spotlight's light, in Ivory or Midnight; **Luxe finish** (Gold, Silver, Bronze). With Luxe on, the color and font pickers step aside. IDX only.
- **Photo & Theme**: **Ivory** / **Midnight** - "Applies everywhere instantly, nothing to re-paste."
- Accent color picker.
- **Font** (email-safe only): **System**, **Classic**, **Editorial Serif**, **Friendly**.

### Previews
- **Email Version** - exactly what's pasted into Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail (calmer animation - "that's normal, not a bug").
- **Site Version** - how your card shows on your web pages: the Guest Pass, CMAs, Spotlights, your IDX site and blogs (big buttons, stacked on phones).
- **Actual Install Preview** shows the real install code's look.

### Smart links (what clicks do)
Links in the signature are worked out when someone clicks, not when you paste: the featured listing opens that home's Listing Spotlight if you've published one (otherwise its listing page); sales open your sales; buttons open the current form; there's also a Save Contact card. So a new Spotlight or listing is picked up without re-copying.

### Install Your Signature (**Install Signature**)
Pick your app, click **Copy HTML**, then paste straight into the app's signature box (not into a notes app first, or it pastes as plain text):
- **Gmail**: Settings (gear) -> See all settings -> General tab -> Signature -> Create new -> click in the box and paste -> **Save Changes**.
- **Outlook web / new**: Settings -> Mail -> Compose and reply -> Email signature -> New signature, name it, paste, set as default for new messages, Save.
- **Outlook desktop** (classic Windows): File -> Options -> Mail -> Signatures -> New, name it, paste, pick it for New messages and Replies/forwards, OK.
- **Apple Mail**: Mail -> Settings -> Signatures -> select your account -> + -> paste -> close Settings (saves automatically).
- The install code is kept under Gmail's 10,000-character signature limit.

### When to re-copy
- No re-copy needed: new listings, sales, stats, your photo, and Ivory/Midnight - "Your featured home and sales update by themselves."
- Re-copy needed: text, colors, and which icons or buttons show ("Re-copy to apply your latest edits.").
- Outlook desktop and Apple Mail keep their own copy of the pictures - after a new photo, listing or sale: "Re-copy to show your new photo, listing or sales."
- After a Branding change while on My Brand Kit, copy it into your email again.

### Other places the signature card appears
Spotlight's Listing Agent section ("My email signature card" switch), the end of blog articles ("End with my email signature"), and your web pages (Guest Pass, CMAs, IDX site).

## Can't do / common mistakes
- Hub didn't change color? Its "Look comes from" isn't on My Brand Kit.
- Luxe (Branding, signature, Studio, forms, invites) needs IDX; others see it as a preview.
- Can't add LinkedIn or custom-URL buttons to the signature - buttons must be your forms.
- More than 3 signature buttons (Agent Hub + 2) won't fit.
- Logos must be JPG or PNG.
- Pasting into Notes/Word first strips the design - paste directly into the email app.
