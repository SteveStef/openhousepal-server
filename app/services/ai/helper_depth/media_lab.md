Media Lab in depth: every photo effect and option, AI video and its title/branding layers, what each plan gets, what it costs from your AI allowance, and how results are saved and downloaded.

## Who gets it
- **IDX**: listing photos from the MLS (tabs **My Listings**, **My Sales**, **Search Any Address**) plus **Upload Photos**.
- **No-MLS Premium**: upload-only - your own photos. Listing photos can't be used ("Your plan can only use uploaded photos here.").
- Locked on Basic, Premium and No-MLS Basic.

## The projects page (Tools > Media Lab)
- Header: "Twilight, virtual staging, lights-on and video - from any listing photo or your own."
- Filters: **All**, **Twilight**, **Staging**, **Lights On**, **AI Video**, **Videos**.
- Each card shows its status: Generating / Ready ("Ready - tap to open") / FAILED. "One of your projects didn't finish - open it to try again."
- Card buttons: **Download** and **Delete**. Click **Create New** to start.

## Making something: 4 steps (Property -> What to make -> Pick photos -> Generate)
1. **Property** - "Pick a property, or upload your own photos". Uploads: JPG, PNG, WebP or iPhone photos; very large ones are shrunk to fit. "Your library stays across sessions, separate from any listing's photos." You can drag and drop.
2. **What do you want to make?**
   - **Photo edit** - "Stack twilight, lights-on, virtual staging & enhancement onto one photo - all applied as a single combined edit."
   - **AI Video** - up to 7 photos -> about a 35-second marketing video, about 5 seconds per photo with a gentle camera move on each.
3. **Pick photos** - tap a photo to pick it; tap again or double-tap to see it bigger ("Use this photo" / "Picked - keep this photo").
4. **Generate** (photo) or **Create video** (video).

## Photo edit effects ("Effects - stack any, applied as one edit")
- **Twilight** - "Daytime exterior -> high-end dusk, lights on". Best on an exterior.
- **Lights On** - "Turn the visible lights on".
- **Virtual Stage** - "Furnish an empty room". Both are required:
  - **Room type**: Living room, Family room, Primary bedroom, Bedroom, Nursery, Kitchen, Dining room, Home office, Finished basement, Bonus / flex room, Home gym, Sunroom, Entryway / foyer, Patio / outdoor.
  - **Style**: Modern, Transitional, Luxury, Traditional.
  - **"Virtually Staged" label** (shown on the photo, saved in downloads): **Off**, **Bottom middle**, **Bottom right**. You can change it later on the result page without re-generating.
- **Enhance** - "Grass, sky, lighting, polish & clean-up". Pick any of:
  - *Enhance*: Improve grass / lawn, Improve / replace sky, Brighten exterior, Improve lighting, General photo polish.
  - *Clean up*: Clean up leaves, sticks & debris; Remove cars, bins & clutter; Remove furniture (chairs, sofas, tables).
  - When you pick a removal option you get **Circle what to remove** (optional, recommended): draw a loose loop around each large object (car, chairs, furniture). "Everything outside your circles stays exactly as it is." **Undo** / **Clear**. Nothing circled = "the AI will find what to remove on its own". Leaves and sticks are cleaned up everywhere on their own.
- A photo edit takes about a minute or so ("Preparing your photo…", "Refining detail & lighting…", "Almost there…").

## AI Video options
- **Style**:
  - **Standard - gentle camera move**: up to 7 photos, shown in the order you pick them. One smooth 1080p video.
  - **Day -> Twilight timelapse**: exactly 1 photo, one clip of about 6 seconds - "the camera holds completely still while daylight fades to dusk." Works best on an exterior with a real sky and windows.
- Use standard landscape photos (roughly 4:3 to 16:9). Very tall or ultra-wide panoramas are refused ("Choose a standard landscape photo for best AI video quality.").
- Videos take several minutes. "Feel free to leave this page - or close OpenHousePal entirely - it keeps working in the background." Your Activity bell lights up when it's ready (and the "AI media ready" email, if on).

## On the result page
- **Photo edits**: **Show staged** / **Show original** before-and-after compare, **"Virtually Staged" label** (Off / Bottom middle / Bottom right), **Project name** (renaming saves on its own), and **Try a different effect** -> **Create new version** (re-runs on the same photo with new effects, room type, style or clean-up). Every run is kept in **Version history**.
- **Videos**: preview and download in four shapes - **Landscape** (YouTube, web, 16:9), **Vertical** (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, 9:16), **Square** (Instagram / Facebook feed, 1:1), **Portrait** (Instagram / Facebook feed, 4:5). "Every format is cropped from the one master render; nothing re-generates." **Copy link** shares it. **Clip name** renames it.
- **Video layers** (optional, saved on their own, burned into the download):
  - **Look & color**: **Signature** ("Bold, modern, colorful") or **Luxe** (black, ivory and your metal), plus a finish/color picker ("Pick any color").
  - **Opening title** - "The big reveal at the start": **Situation** (Just Listed, Open House, Coming Soon, Price Improved, Under Contract, Just Sold, Back on Market, New Construction, Custom), **Title**, **Small line above**, **Day & time** (open house), **Previous price**, **Where it sits** (Top / Middle / Bottom), **Property info** (Address, Town, Price, Beds, Baths, Sq ft).
  - **Corner tag** (position: Top left, Top middle, Bottom left...).
  - **Your branding** - "Photo, name & brokerage badge".
  - **Closing card** - "You, your contact & a call to action", with a **Call to action** line (e.g. "Schedule your private tour").
  - **Timeline**: drag each layer's bar or ends (they snap to clip changes); **Leaves by** / **Ends the video**; **Animate** (plays its exit animation), stays to the last frame, or disappears instantly.
  - With layers on, the download renders them in real time: "Plays through once in real time - keep this tab open."
- **Delete** ("Delete this project?") removes the image or video from your storage for good. "The original property photo is not touched."

## AI allowance (cost) - what agents see
- Every run uses part of your **monthly AI allowance** - the same pool as CMAs, blog writing and other AI tools. Before you press Generate you see "Estimated: about X% of this month's AI usage (you're at Y% now)", plus a usage meter. Videos show "~X% of your monthly allowance" as you add photos.
- Videos cost far more than photo edits, and more photos = more cost. A timelapse is a single clip.
- If a run would use up the rest: "This may use up the rest of your monthly allowance."
- When you've used most of it: "AI Studio photo and video are paused until it resets" (on their billing day). CMAs and cheat sheets still work; the AI helper chat has its own separate allowance.
- A run that fails isn't charged.
- **Storage**: results count toward your media storage. When it's full, generating stops: "You've used your [X] GB of media storage. Delete some photos, videos, blog images or designs to free space, then try again."

## Where results go / reuse
- Everything stays in your Media Lab projects until you delete it.
- Spotlight's **AI video tour** step can use any Media Lab video of that home ("Make a full AI video tour of this home" opens Media Lab).

## Can't do / common mistakes
- No-MLS Premium can't pull MLS listing photos - upload them.
- Virtual Stage won't start without a room type and a style.
- Timelapse takes exactly one photo; picking more trims to one.
- More than 7 photos isn't possible in one video.
- There's no "re-generate" for videos on the result page - make a new one from Create New. Changing the video's shape or layers never re-generates.
- Staging should be disclosed - use the "Virtually Staged" label where your MLS requires it.
- Deleting can't be undone.

## Cinematic Video (its own editor, /studio/media/motion/<id>)
- New option on "What do you want to make?": a film-style tour with real 3D camera moves through the home's own photos (no AI video model, nothing invented), room captions, their branding and music. With a listing picked it takes all of the home's photos and opens its editor straight away; with uploads they pick the photos it may use, then **Continue**.
- (Up to 40 of the home's photos are sorted; a video uses up to 9.)
- Saved as a draft from the first tap - every change saves by itself ("Saved" top right). The projects page shows "Draft - tap to continue" until it's made.
- A live preview of the real video goes with the steps - every change shows instantly, free. A tall video (Vertical, Portrait) sits beside the steps on a computer; a wide one (Landscape, Square) sits above them; on a phone it's always above. Before rooms are picked it plays their look on example photos. **See an example** plays a 3-photo sample.
- 8 steps:
  1. **Length & shape** - **15 sec** (up to 3 photos, quick teaser), **30 sec** (up to 5, Reels & stories, most popular), **45 sec** (up to 7), **60 sec** (up to 9) - each room stays on screen about 5-6 seconds; shape **Landscape**, **Vertical**, **Square** or **Portrait**. Other shapes can be added after it's made (each made fresh, a small share of their monthly AI).
  2. **What to show** - the AI sorts all the photos into rooms (about 15-40 seconds, "Sorting your photos into rooms"); only rooms the home has photos of are shown, each with how many photos it has. They tap the rooms they want, up to the length's limit ("That's the most for 30 seconds - take one out, or pick a longer video."). **Our pick** puts back the suggestion. **Don't see something? Add photos**. The best photo of each room is used, plus a second photo of the strongest rooms when the length has time.
  3. **Check each photo** - one photo at a time: "Is this the kitchen?" -> **Yes, looks right**, **Show another** (the next photo of that room), or **Take it out**. They can edit the caption and the small line above it, switch the caption off, or fix the room under **Wrong room?**. A photo the AI wasn't sure about says so. **They all look right - skip ahead** checks them all.
  4. **What's it for?** - Just Listed, Coming Soon, Open House (asks day & time), Price Improved (old price crossed out), Under Contract, Just Sold, Back on Market, New Construction, Private Listing, Custom. It sets the opening words and the closing card.
  5. **Look & motion** - theme **Default** (ivory type and an accent color from the usual 12) or **Luxe** (black, ivory and Gold / Silver / Bronze); animation style **Cinematic** (recommended), **Glide** or **Bold** (Reels/TikTok).
  6. **Order & transitions** - one transition for the whole video (Clean cut, Crossfade, Dip to black, Light leak, Whip pan, Zoom through - each card previews itself); any single gap can use its own; a camera move per room (Auto, Push in, Slide left/right, Rise up, Orbit, Drone drift).
  7. **Words & details** - opening title and line, address/town/price, beds/baths/sq ft/acres, room captions on/off, closing card and call to action, listing credit (keep it when it isn't their listing), branding (Off / Opening only / Whole video - photo, name, brokerage, phone, like the sign-in sheet), music (**Elegant piano** - piano and soft strings, the default; **Bright & modern** - acoustic guitar and light drums, more upbeat; **No music** - each has a play button to listen first, and the live preview plays it too).
  8. **Make your video** - a summary of the shape and length (**Change** goes back to step 1), then **Make my video**. Every photo is sharpened with AI automatically (no switch; a tiny share of their monthly AI, once per photo). Making the video itself also uses a small share of their monthly AI (the Make step shows it - longer videos use a little more). If their AI for the month is used up, new videos and updates wait until it resets (the Make button says so); finished videos still play and save. Progress shows a **"Ready in about"** countdown clock (worked out from how fast recent videos were made, plus any videos ahead in line - "2 videos ahead of yours" when others are being made first). They can close the page, log out or turn the computer off - the video keeps being made. The Activity bell says the moment it's ready, and their next daily briefing lists it (there's no separate "ready" email for Cinematic Videos). After a video is made they can add other shapes (Landscape, Vertical, Square, Portrait - a few minutes each, a small share of their monthly AI each); if they change something and press **Update my video**, the other shapes they'd made are re-made by themselves right after. If they pick **Open House** and the listing already has an open house scheduled (on the MLS or in their Open House Manager), the day and time are filled in for them. Their branding badge shows their photo, name, brokerage, phone and email (all editable on Words & details; phone and email start from their Agent Hub contact details). If the screen says **The video maker is restarting**, the Make / Update / extra-shape buttons wait (greyed) and turn back on by themselves within a minute; saving shapes already made still works.
- When it's ready: **Save** downloads; other shapes say **Make it** (a few minutes, a small share of their monthly AI). **Sections** lets them **Remove** / **Put back** the opening title, any room, captions, stats, closing card or branding, then **Preview your changes** and **Update my video**. Previewing changes is free; each update or extra shape is a new video, so it uses a small share of their monthly AI.
- AI usage: sorting the photos into rooms (with captions) and the automatic sharpening together use well under 1% of the monthly AI allowance - the Make step shows the exact share before they press the button. The 3D depth for each photo is free. Making the video (and each update or extra shape) uses a small share too - the Make step shows the exact share. It all counts on the AI usage bar but never uses up the AI Video limit.
- From the chat: the AI Media card has a **Cinematic Video** tile. With a listing named it uses all of the home's photos (no picking on the card); with uploads they pick the photos it may use. They press **Make it** and the whole editor opens right inside the chat (full width on a computer) - every step above, then **Make my video**. The expand button top right opens it full screen. Same plans as Media Lab: IDX (listing photos or uploads) and No-MLS Premium (uploads only).

**From the chat.** Everything here except circling areas to remove and the video's title cards can be started in the chat: drop a photo in (or name a listing), and the card asks the tool, the photo and its choices. It uses the same monthly AI allowance, keeps going if they leave, and the finished photo or video shows on the card and in Media Lab.
