Media Lab in depth: every photo effect and option, AI video and its title/branding layers, what each plan gets, what it costs from your AI allowance, and how results are saved and downloaded.

## Who gets it
- **IDX**: listing photos from the MLS (tabs **My Listings**, **My Sales**, **Search Any Address**) plus **Upload Photos**.
- **No-MLS Premium**: upload-only - your own photos. Listing photos can't be used ("Your plan can only use uploaded photos here.").
- Locked on Basic, Premium and No-MLS Basic.

## The projects page (Tools > Media Lab)
- Header: "Twilight, virtual staging, lights-on and video - from any listing photo or your own."
- Filters: **All**, **Twilight**, **Staging**, **Lights On**, **AI Video**, **Videos**.
- Each card shows its status: Generating / Ready ("Ready - tap to open") / FAILED. "One of your projects didn't finish - open it to try again."
- Card buttons: **Download** and **Delete**. Click **Create New** to start.

## Making something: 4 steps (Property -> What to make -> Pick photos -> Generate)
1. **Property** - "Pick a property, or upload your own photos". Uploads: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 12MB each. "Your library stays across sessions, separate from any listing's photos." You can drag and drop.
2. **What do you want to make?**
   - **Photo edit** - "Stack twilight, lights-on, virtual staging & enhancement onto one photo - all applied as a single combined edit."
   - **AI Video** - up to 7 photos -> about a 35-second marketing video, about 5 seconds per photo with a gentle camera move on each.
3. **Pick photos** - tap a photo to pick it; tap again or double-tap to see it bigger ("Use this photo" / "Picked - keep this photo").
4. **Generate** (photo) or **Create video** (video).

## Photo edit effects ("Effects - stack any, applied as one edit")
- **Twilight** - "Daytime exterior -> high-end dusk, lights on". Best on an exterior.
- **Lights On** - "Turn the visible lights on".
- **Virtual Stage** - "Furnish an empty room". Both are required:
  - **Room type**: Living room, Family room, Primary bedroom, Bedroom, Nursery, Kitchen, Dining room, Home office, Finished basement, Bonus / flex room, Home gym, Sunroom, Entryway / foyer, Patio / outdoor.
  - **Style**: Modern, Transitional, Luxury, Traditional.
  - **"Virtually Staged" label** (shown on the photo, saved in downloads): **Off**, **Bottom middle**, **Bottom right**. You can change it later on the result page without re-generating.
- **Enhance** - "Grass, sky, lighting, polish & clean-up". Pick any of:
  - *Enhance*: Improve grass / lawn, Improve / replace sky, Brighten exterior, Improve lighting, General photo polish.
  - *Clean up*: Clean up leaves, sticks & debris; Remove cars, bins & clutter; Remove furniture (chairs, sofas, tables).
  - When you pick a removal option you get **Circle what to remove** (optional, recommended): draw a loose loop around each large object (car, chairs, furniture). "Everything outside your circles stays exactly as it is." **Undo** / **Clear**. Nothing circled = "the AI will find what to remove on its own". Leaves and sticks are cleaned up everywhere on their own.
- A photo edit takes about a minute or so ("Preparing your photo…", "Refining detail & lighting…", "Almost there…").

## AI Video options
- **Style**:
  - **Standard - gentle camera move**: up to 7 photos, shown in the order you pick them. One smooth 1080p video.
  - **Day -> Twilight timelapse**: exactly 1 photo, one clip of about 6 seconds - "the camera holds completely still while daylight fades to dusk." Works best on an exterior with a real sky and windows.
- Use standard landscape photos (roughly 4:3 to 16:9). Very tall or ultra-wide panoramas are refused ("Choose a standard landscape photo for best AI video quality.").
- Videos take several minutes. "Feel free to leave this page - or close OpenHousePal entirely - it keeps working in the background." Your Activity bell lights up when it's ready (and the "AI media ready" email, if on).

## On the result page
- **Photo edits**: **Show staged** / **Show original** before-and-after compare, **"Virtually Staged" label** (Off / Bottom middle / Bottom right), **Project name** (renaming saves on its own), and **Try a different effect** -> **Create new version** (re-runs on the same photo with new effects, room type, style or clean-up). Every run is kept in **Version history**.
- **Videos**: preview and download in four shapes - **Landscape** (YouTube, web, 16:9), **Vertical** (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, 9:16), **Square** (Instagram / Facebook feed, 1:1), **Portrait** (Instagram / Facebook feed, 4:5). "Every format is cropped from the one master render; nothing re-generates." **Copy link** shares it. **Clip name** renames it.
- **Video layers** (optional, saved on their own, burned into the download):
  - **Look & color**: **Signature** ("Bold, modern, colorful") or **Luxe** (black, ivory and your metal), plus a finish/color picker ("Pick any color").
  - **Opening title** - "The big reveal at the start": **Situation** (Just Listed, Open House, Coming Soon, Price Improved, Under Contract, Just Sold, Back on Market, New Construction, Custom), **Title**, **Small line above**, **Day & time** (open house), **Previous price**, **Where it sits** (Top / Middle / Bottom), **Property info** (Address, Town, Price, Beds, Baths, Sq ft).
  - **Corner tag** (position: Top left, Top middle, Bottom left...).
  - **Your branding** - "Photo, name & brokerage badge".
  - **Closing card** - "You, your contact & a call to action", with a **Call to action** line (e.g. "Schedule your private tour").
  - **Timeline**: drag each layer's bar or ends (they snap to clip changes); **Leaves by** / **Ends the video**; **Animate** (plays its exit animation), stays to the last frame, or disappears instantly.
  - With layers on, the download renders them in real time: "Plays through once in real time - keep this tab open."
- **Delete** ("Delete this project?") removes the image or video from your storage for good. "The original property photo is not touched."

## AI allowance (cost) - what agents see
- Every run uses part of your **monthly AI allowance** - the same pool as CMAs, blog writing and other AI tools. Before you press Generate you see "Estimated: about X% of this month's AI usage (you're at Y% now)", plus a usage meter. Videos show "~X% of your monthly allowance" as you add photos.
- Videos cost far more than photo edits, and more photos = more cost. A timelapse is a single clip.
- If a run would use up the rest: "This may use up the rest of your monthly allowance."
- When you've used most of it: "AI Studio photo & video are paused until it resets on the 1st. CMAs, cheat sheets and chat still work."
- A run that fails isn't charged.
- **Storage**: results count toward your media storage. When it's full, generating stops: "You've used your [X] GB of media storage. Delete some photos, videos, blog images or designs to free space, then try again."

## Where results go / reuse
- Everything stays in your Media Lab projects until you delete it.
- Spotlight's **AI video tour** step can use any Media Lab video of that home ("Make a full AI video tour of this home" opens Media Lab).

## Can't do / common mistakes
- No-MLS Premium can't pull MLS listing photos - upload them.
- Virtual Stage won't start without a room type and a style.
- Timelapse takes exactly one photo; picking more trims to one.
- More than 7 photos isn't possible in one video.
- There's no "re-generate" for videos on the result page - make a new one from Create New. Changing the video's shape or layers never re-generates.
- Staging should be disclosed - use the "Virtually Staged" label where your MLS requires it.
- Deleting can't be undone.
