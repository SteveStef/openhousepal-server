Leads and follow-ups in depth: the Leads list and a lead's page, statuses, tags, notes, per-lead alerts, every automatic follow-up situation with its timing, styles and calendar alerts, tap-to-text, the Email Tracker rules, Activity, and what the daily briefing contains.

## Who gets it
- Leads, Follow-ups, Email Tracker and Activity are on every plan.
- Some automatic follow-up situations need a plan feature: Showcase situations on Premium and IDX, Website sign-in on IDX, Unhappy review on No-MLS Premium, Premium and IDX. The Follow-ups page only lists situations your plan can trigger.

## The Leads list (Leads → Leads, "Every lead, one place")
- One lead per person, built from open house sign-ins, forms, invites, Showcases, Spotlight and listing pages, your website, portal emails and reviews.
- Tabs: **Needs you**, **No agent**, **New**, **Contacted**, **In talks**, **Signed**, **All**, **Has agent**, **Hidden**.
  - Needs you = a follow-up due today or overdue, or a request waiting (book a showing, send a disclosure, reply to a comment, update a Showcase search). A follow-up set for a later day doesn't count.
  - No agent = everyone without an agent, plus anyone with an agent you left a note on.
- Search "Search name, email, phone or open house". Filters **Tags** (only tags you added) and **Came in from** (Open houses - then pick which open house -, Showcases, Forms, Spotlights & listings, Website & blog, Zillow & portals, Reviews, Other).
- Order: Action needed first, Warmest first, Latest activity, Newest leads, Name A-Z, Opens my emails most, Opens my emails least.
- **Export** downloads the leads you're looking at as a CSV. Header shows "Need you" and "New" for This week / Last 30 days / Last 3 months / This year / All time.
- Row actions: **Call**, **Text**, **Copy email**, **Star** (stars the lead and their Showcase together), **Mark that you reached out**, and "..." → **Move to** New/Contacted/In talks/Signed, **Mark contacted**, **Mark as seen**, **Hide** / **Show again**, **Delete**. **Mark all seen** clears every red dot at once (can't be undone; follow-ups and requests stay in Needs you).

## Warmth (Hot / Warm / Cool)
- Every action earns points: asking to see a home most, then questions, sign-ins and forms, then likes and comments; views and email opens a little. Recent activity counts most; after 30 quiet days the points are gone. Hot is 40+, Warm 15+, below that Cool. Browsing or opening emails alone can't make someone Hot. Your own previews never count.

## Inside one lead
- **Edit** name, email or phone. Changing the email moves their Showcase, sign-ins and queued emails to the new address and restarts that address's email rules. If they have a Showcase you're asked **Send a new invite?** → **No thanks** or **Yes, send one** → **Showcase from an open house** or **Normal Showcase invite**. Two leads can't share one email.
- **Where they stand**: New ("Haven't talked yet"), Contacted ("You've reached out"), In talks ("Actively working together"), Signed ("Your client").
- **Do they have an agent?**: No agent ("Free to work with you"), Not sure ("Haven't asked yet"), Has an agent (moves them to the Has agent tab; most nurture follow-ups stop for them, but direct requests - disclosure, showings, questions, search changes - still get follow-ups).
- **How they found you**: Came from, First touch, Latest, "What they told you", **See it in Activity**.
- **Waiting on you**: each open request with **Open** and **Done** ("I've handled this - clear it").
- **Follow-ups** with **Alert settings** and **All follow-ups**; **Their Showcase** (Homes/Liked/Passed, last active, Showcase emails on/off, **Open**, **Their link**); **Emails** (sent, opened, clicked, automatic emails on/weekly/off); **Activity** (Everything, Forms & requests, Showcase); **Open houses they came to**.
- **Notes**: "Add a note..." - also shows on their open house sign-in and on their follow-ups and calendar events; edit or delete your own notes. A note on a guest who has an agent gives you a "Guest with an agent + your note" heads-up instead of a pitch.
- **Tags**: "Add a tag..." then +; x removes. Portal leads arrive tagged "Portal Lead".
- **Record**: Lead since, Last contacted, Last active, **Mark contacted** / **Contacted them again** (checking off a follow-up does this for you).
- **Hide** ("Take them off your lists - nothing is deleted"; also clears their open follow-ups, which don't come back when shown again). **Delete** removes them for good: activity, notes, Activity feed, Email Tracker and their Showcase; automatic emails stop. No undo.

## Per-lead alert switches (Alert settings, "Just for [name]")
- **Put [name]'s follow-ups on my calendar**: off asks "Still want [name]'s follow-ups in OpenHousePal?" → **Yes, keep them here** (Follow-ups page and Dash only) or **No, stop them** (no new follow-ups for this person).
- **Email me when [name] likes or comments**: off = still in their activity, no email.
- The same switches show on the "Mark as done" page opened from a calendar alert.

## Follow-ups page (Leads → Follow-ups, "Never miss a follow-up")
- Master switch **Follow-ups On/Off**: off = "No automatic follow-ups"; nothing new is made and follow-ups are hidden from Dash, Leads and the daily email. Anything already on your calendar stays. Settings are kept. Good for agents whose CRM already reminds them.
- **Add** your own: "What needs doing?", **When** (Today, Tomorrow, In 3 days, Next Monday, **Pick a day…**, **Someday**), optional **Add a time** (with a time it buzzes your phone; without, it's all day) → **Add follow-up**.
- Cards: **Mark done** / **Undo - mark not done** (done also marks the person contacted and resolves a linked request), "..." → **Snooze** (Tomorrow, In 3 days, Next week - keeps its time of day) or delete, ready-to-send message (**Text**, **Copy email**, copy). Showing requests add **Book in ShowingTime** with the listing agent's phone, email and MLS#, then **Mark scheduled**. **Suggested from what they just did** → **Add for tomorrow**. **Inbox** = every follow-up added in the last two weeks.
- One open automatic follow-up per person - no duplicates. Direct requests (disclosure, second visit, showing, search change) always get their own. Missed follow-ups move to today each morning for up to a week.

## Automatic follow-up situations
Grouped as **Open houses**, **Form responses**, **Showcases, listings & reviews**. Default timing in brackets.
- **Open house sign-in** [Next day] - every guest without an agent, plus any guest you leave a note on.
- **Similar Homes QR scan** [Next day] - a guest without an agent scans the Similar Homes handout QR.
- **Second visit request** [Right away] - quotes the day and time they picked.
- **Disclosure request** [Right away] - from Guest Pass, Cheat Sheet QR or the second-visit form; their email on its own line plus a short text to send once you've emailed it.
- **Setup My Own Showcase** [Next day] (Premium/IDX).
- **I'm Buying form** [Right away] - a Showcase is built quietly from their answers on Showcase plans.
- **How Can We Help form** [Right away] - separate buyer and seller messages; sellers flagged as a SELLER LEAD.
- **Any other form** [Right away] - onboarding, seller, home value, custom forms, and blog readers' questions.
- **Invite RSVP** [Next day] - RSVPs (or "can't make it but interested") from people without an agent.
- **Showing request** [Right away] - from a Showcase, Spotlight or the Schedule a Showing form; quotes every time they picked.
- **Showcase comment** [Next day] (Premium/IDX) - alert only.
- **Showcase likes** [Next day] (Premium/IDX) - all homes liked that day on one follow-up the next morning, naming the homes.
- **Showcase search change** [Right away] (Premium/IDX) - alert only.
- **Listing inquiry** [Right away] - Spotlight questions and Zillow/Realtor.com/portal leads (non-IDX plans: portal leads only).
- **Website sign-in** [Next day] (IDX) - someone taps "Continue with Google" on your website; once per person.
- **Unhappy review (1-3 stars)** [Right away] (review plans) - alert only.

Each situation has:
- On/off ("Off - nothing is added for this situation").
- **When**: **Soon** (30 minutes to about 2½ hours after, within 8 AM-5 PM; outside those hours, first thing the next working morning), **In 2 hrs** (about 2 hours after, until 8 PM, otherwise next morning), **Next day** (sometime 8 AM-5 PM tomorrow). Times are Eastern. Timed follow-ups pick a free quarter-hour, skipping busy times on your connected calendar and other follow-ups.
- **Style**: **Set time** (buzzes your phone) or **All day** (on that day's or the next day's calendar, no buzz). Both show as Free so they never block your calendar.
- **Message**: edit the ready-to-send text with drag-in pills that fill in per lead; parts in [BRACKETS] (like [ADD DATE & TIME]) are for you to fill in. Every message ends with your sign-off (name and brokerage from Settings). **Reset to default**. Situations that build a Showcase also have a **Showcase pitch** part ("reminder" or "intro"). Alert-only situations (comment, search change, unhappy review) have nothing to edit.

## Calendar alerts for follow-ups
- "How follow-ups show on your calendar": buzz 0, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes before (default 15) and **Again at the time** (default on). Outlook allows one buzz per event, so it uses the first.
- Follow-ups only reach your phone once a calendar is connected (**Connect your calendar** / **Reconnect your calendar**). They go on the "OpenHousePal" calendar.
- Each event carries links: **Mark as done** (checks it off and crosses it out; "Did you also do these?" lists today's others; **Undo - not done yet**), **Text [name] from your phone**, and **Save to contacts** (their contact card with notes).

## Tap-to-text
- **Text [name] from your phone** opens a page where **Text** opens your own Messages app with their number and the suggested text filled in (sent from your real cell). **Email** opens your mail app instead. Either one checks the follow-up off. On a computer you get **Copy text** and a QR code to scan with your phone.

## Email Tracker (Leads → Email Tracker, "Every email, tracked")
- Last 30 days: **Sent**, **Opened**, **Clicked**, **Slowed**. Tabs **People** / **Sent emails**. Filters All, On, Weekly, Off, Not in your leads; sort Latest email, Most engaged, Least engaged, Name A-Z.
- Rules: unopened on 3 different days → **Weekly only**; then 3 skipped weekly emails → **Emails off** (their Showcase may fall asleep). Opening any email or using their Showcase turns daily back on by itself.
- Personal emails (sign-in thank-you, tour confirmation, your comment reply, a Showcase you send) always go.
- **Turn off** / **Turn back on** per person (a manual off sticks). Unsubscribed, bounced or spam-marked people can't be switched back on by you.

## Activity (Leads → Activity)
- Tabs: All, Unread, Opens, Forms, Tours, Questions, Showcases, Emails, Follow-ups, Reviews, Hosting, Account. Search, type, date range, Newest/Oldest first, **Clear**. Tap an item to mark it read. **Mark all read**. No mark-unread.

## The daily briefing
- Email "Daily briefing" (turn it on/off and pick **Arrives at**, 6:00 AM-9:00 PM Eastern in 15-minute steps, default 4:30 PM, in Tools → Email). On the Dash it's **Today's Rundown**.
- Contains: follow-ups due today and overdue (top 5, "+ N more"); things waiting on you (agents asking to host your listing, hosting answers needed, leads needing a response, form responses you haven't opened, a support reply); client activity (clients asking for a search change, clients who changed their own search); new matches and price drops across your clients' Showcases; and open house wrap-ups (guests from opens that have ended).
- Skipped on days with nothing new. If you turn the briefing off, open house wrap-ups still arrive as their own email.
- On No-MLS plans the Showcase and hosting parts never appear, and the Daily briefing row isn't listed among the inbox switches.

## Can't do / common mistakes
- No "add lead" button - leads come from sign-ins, forms, invites, Showcases (Premium/IDX: creating a Showcase for someone adds them) and portal emails.
- No calendar color choice on screen; follow-up events use one gold color.
- Can't mark Activity items unread; can't undo Mark all seen or Delete.
