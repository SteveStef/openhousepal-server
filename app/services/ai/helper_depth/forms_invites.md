Forms & Invites in depth: every premade form, the custom form builder and its question types, looks, after-submit choices, campaigns and QR codes, stats and results; and invites - kinds, steps, designs, animation, RSVP questions, extra days, sending and reminders.

## Who gets it
- Every plan. The **Setup My Own Showcase** form ("Showcase Request") needs a Showcase plan - Premium or IDX ("Upgrade to Premium to turn it on").
- The **Luxe** form look and Luxe invite metals are IDX only.
- **Open House Requests** and **Similar Listings QR** come from your open house Cheat Sheets (MLS plans).

## The page (Tools > Forms & Invites)
- Switch at the top: **Forms** ("Capture leads") / **Invites** ("Events & RSVPs").
- Forms header: **Form Views**, **Forms Filled**, **Completion Rate**, **Inbox** ("Every form response and Cheat Sheet request, newest first"; "All caught up" when empty).
- Invites header: **Live invites**, **Guests coming**, **Times opened**, and **RSVPs** (new ones you haven't looked at).

## Premade forms ("Built for every situation - share the link or QR and every answer lands in Results")
- **I'm Buying** - basic info from a new buyer lead.
- **I'm Selling** - basic info from a potential seller.
- **What's My Home Worth?** - the homeowner's property info so you can prepare a value estimate.
- **Schedule a Showing** - someone requesting to tour a specific property (you confirm the time from the result - see below).
- **How Can We Help** - a general form for all leads (bios, YouTube descriptions...).
- **Seller Onboarding** / **Buyer Onboarding** - fuller intakes once they're your client.
- **Make Me Sell** - for mailers: what it would take for a homeowner to sell.
- **Setup My Own Showcase** (Premium/IDX) - open house guests scan a QR on your Brag Sheet to build their own Showcase of matching homes with alerts.
- Each card: on/off switch, **Share** (Open link / Copy link, OpenHousePal Link and Website Link), **Download QR code**, **View stats**, **Edit**, **Duplicate** ("Duplicate this form into an editable copy" -> "Copy created in Custom Forms"), and responses.
- To change a premade form's questions, Duplicate it. **Revert to default** resets title, questions, wording and settings to the original ("Your link and on/off state stay the same." Can't be undone.)

## Custom forms
- **New custom form** ("Start from scratch - any questions you like"). Every custom form starts with Full name, Email and Phone - locked, can't be removed.
- **Choose a question type**: **Short answer** (a single line), **Long answer** (a paragraph), **Single choice** (pick one), **Multiple choice** (pick multiple), **Number**, **Currency** (a dollar amount), **Date**, **Time**, **Email** (validated), **Phone**, **Property Address** (address lookup with live Google suggestions), **Statement** (a transition slide, no input).
- Each question: **Question label**, **Type**, **Required** (not on Statements), **Placeholder**, **Helper text (optional)**, **Choices** (for choice questions), **Unit suffix (optional)** for numbers (e.g. "square feet"). Drag to reorder; delete with the trash.
- No "if this, then that" branching in the custom builder.
- Made from the AI helper chat: the card's steps are **Questions** (the helper's questions for this form, each with its answer type and choices - the x takes one off; **Ideas for this form** made for what the form is about; **Common ones** for that kind of form (sellers, buyers, events, being featured, renters), **+N more**; or type their own; **Done**), the **Moving background** switch and **About a home?** (No, or one of their own active listings with its photo). Full name, email and phone are always on the form.
- An invite made from the AI helper chat: the card shows the invite itself, small, redrawn as they tap the steps - **What kind** (Open house, Broker's open, Client event, Other event), **Home** (their listings - its photo becomes the cover; or Not one of my listings), **Day** (the next week as taps, or Another day), **Time** (11-1, 12-2, 1-3, 2-4, 5-7 or exact times), **Design** (Classic, Garden, Midnight, Modern) and **Color** (My brand, Luxe Gold / Silver / Bronze on IDX, or a color). **Make it** saves it to Forms & Invites; nothing is sent until they send it there.
- The invite's place NAME is for a venue (a club, a restaurant). An invite never shows the street twice - a place name that is just the street, or a title that already says the address, is left off the card's extra lines.
- Builder sections: **Welcome Screen** (**Intro headline**, **Subtitle**), questions, **Thank You Screen** (**Success heading**, **Success message**), **Settings**, with a **Live preview**.
- Each form has its own link - **Edit URL** to set the ending (letters, numbers, hyphens; it checks the link is free).

## Form settings
- **Title** and **Description** (shown in your Forms list, clipped after two lines).
- **Show your photo & name** - turn off to keep the form unbranded / anonymous.
- **Linked property (optional)** - brand the form around one listing. **Cycle listing photos** flips through up to 10 of its photos on the welcome screen, 3 seconds each.
- **Agent & property placement**: **Property top, agent bottom** (default), **Agent top, property bottom**, or **Integrated card** ("One cohesive card, like Open House sign-in").
- **After they submit**: **Go to my Agent Hub** (a few seconds after the thank-you), or stay on the thank-you. Showcase forms: **Open their new Showcase**.
- **Background effects** (soft animated graphics behind the form) and **Welcome text effects** (title, words and Start button glide in).
- **Look**: where it comes from - **My Brand Kit** (default), **Match my Agent Hub**, or **Choose my own** ("Its own look": a style, color and Ivory or Midnight). Styles: **Classic** ("Clean, in your color") or **Luxe** ("Metal shine and a serif title" - IDX only), with a metal finish. **Ivory** (light & airy) or **Midnight** (dark & dramatic). **Use this look on all my forms** applies it everywhere.

## Campaigns (tracking links)
- "Same form, new tracking link - track a mailer, ad, or flyer separately." Identical questions and design, its own link, QR code and stats.
- **New Campaign** -> **Campaign Name** (e.g. "September Postcard", "Facebook Ad") -> after-submit page: **My Agent Hub**, **My Sales & Listings**, **Stay on the thank-you screen**, or **My own link** (a full web address). The form's own setting shows as "Form's setting".
- **All Campaigns** window: **General** ("Your everyday link"), each campaign's link and QR, "After submitting: X · Change" (changing it affects already-printed QR codes right away), tabs **Campaigns**, **Stats**, **Results**, and **Delete campaign**.
- Studio designs can make a campaign for each piece automatically.

## Stats (per form or per campaign)
- Range: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **All time**. Eastern time, updates live.
- Totals: **Views**, **Started**, **Shared contact**, **Submitted**, **Time to finish**.
- **Activity** (by day), **How far people get**, **Where people drop off** (every question - who saw it and who left; "Making it optional or moving it later usually helps"), **When people drop off** (day and Morning/Afternoon/Evening), **Where visits come from** (QR scans vs links), **Devices**, **By campaign**, **Top referring sites**.

## Results / responses
- Filters **New** / **Opened**; **View Submission**, **Mark Reviewed**, previous/next, **Delete submission**.
- Schedule a Showing results: pick the requested time (**Top pick**, "Busy on your calendar" times crossed out) or **Another time you agreed on**, then **Confirm this one**.
- **Unfinished responses**: people who left contact info but didn't submit ("Left contact info" / "Filling it out now"), with source (QR scan / Link / Website) and device. **Add to Leads** adds them (and a follow-up unless they said they have an agent or you've turned follow-ups off); **Copy all**; hide from the list.
- Every submitted form becomes a Lead.

## Open House Requests and Similar Listings QR (MLS plans)
- "Request Seller Disclosure" and "Schedule a Second Visit" scans from your Cheat Sheets show up here - no setup.
- Every Similar Properties handout has a "See more like this?" QR - scanning builds the visitor a free Showcase.

## Invites
**Kinds** ("Who are you inviting?"): **Open House** (buyers, neighbors and clients tour the home), **Broker's Open** (agents preview before the public), **Client Event / Dinner**, **Custom** (a market talk, happy hour, class).

**The 7 steps**:
1. **Kind of invite** - "What are you inviting people to?" Also **Who are you inviting?**: **Clients & the public** ("Their RSVPs go to your Leads") or **Other agents** ("Kept out of your Leads").
2. **The event** - "Tell them about it": **Is it about one of your homes?** (optional - pick a listing or **No home**), **Event name** (also the big title, up to 140 characters), **A note from you** (up to 2,000 characters).
3. **The days** - "Which day - or days?" Suggestions: this home's scheduled open houses ("Your open house", "From Bright MLS"), or coming weekends / weekdays / evenings; **Pick other days** for a calendar. Up to 7 days in one invite.
4. **The time** - **Popular times** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening blocks) or **Pick your own time** ("It starts at" + "For how long"); **Same time every day** or a time per day.
5. **The place** - **Address** and optional **Name of the place** (e.g. "Our office"). Guests get a **Get directions** button.
6. **The look** - "Make it yours":
   - **Design**: **Classic** (ivory card, fine border), **Garden** (soft botanicals), **Midnight** (dark, metallic accents), **Modern** (big photo, bold type).
   - **Color**: your Brand Kit color, a palette, any color, or **Luxe** metals (IDX plan).
   - **Animation**: **Normal** ("A paper envelope that opens to the card") or **Luxe** ("Lacquer, foil and light - a cinematic reveal").
   - **Cover photo**: from the home's photos or your own upload (JPG under 10 MB); drag up/down to position. No photo = your initials.
   - **Words on the invitation**: **Line 1 - the big title**, **Line 2 - under the title** (leave empty for none; defaults to the address).
7. **Questions** - "What should guests answer?" Always asked: **Will you be there?** (Yes · Maybe · Can't make it), name, email or phone, and whether they're an agent or working with one ("so only real leads get a follow-up"). Add up to 12 more, from ready ones (How many of you are coming?, Your brokerage, Any dietary needs?, Names of your guests, Are you looking to…, When are you hoping to move?, Do you have buyers who might be a fit?, Will you stay for lunch?, Bringing little ones?, How did you hear about this?, Anything else we should know?) or your own: **Pick one**, **Pick any**, **Short answer**, **Paragraph**, **Number**.
Then **Create my invite** -> "Your invite is ready".

**Sharing**: **Copy** link, **Text**, **Email a list** (paste up to 100 addresses, any format - each gets a short note from you with the link), **QR code** (**Save QR code** - "Print it on a flyer or a sign"), **Open**.

**Managing**: each invite card shows coming / maybe / can't counts. Inside: **Coming**, **Maybe**, **Can't**, **Opened**; filters; **Answers**; tags like New lead, Has an agent, Still interested; **Open in Leads**. Buttons: **Share**, **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Close RSVPs** / **Reopen RSVPs**, **Remind** (emails everyone you emailed who hasn't answered - not more than once per 12 hours, and not while RSVPs are closed), **Delete** ("The link stops working and its guest list goes. People who became Leads stay in Leads.").

**What happens on an RSVP**: client/public invites make or update a Lead; broker's opens never do. You get a bell alert and a "New RSVP" email, plus a "tomorrow" email the day before each day (who's coming, maybes, requests). Guests get a confirmation note from you (from your own mailbox if connected) with a calendar invite when they're coming, and can change their answer any time.

## Can't do / common mistakes
- Can't remove Full name, Email or Phone from a custom form.
- Premade questions can't be edited in place - Duplicate first.
- Changing a campaign's after-submit page changes it for QR codes already printed.
- Luxe form look and Luxe invite metals need IDX.
- Remind does nothing for people you only texted or shared the link with - it only reaches people you emailed from the invite.
- More than 7 days or 12 extra questions isn't possible in one invite.

## From the AI helper chat - follow-through (11/01)
- **Read answers**: "What did the Smiths put on the buyer form?" - every answer, by person and form, newest first. Also who started a form but didn't finish.
- **Who's coming**: an invite's Coming, Maybe, Can't and No answer yet, with counts and what each person answered.
- **Mark reviewed** (one, several, or all of a form's new ones) - clears the Inbox and the red number. Can also mark one as new again.
- **Duplicate a form** - makes an editable copy in Custom Forms (the way to change a premade form's questions).
- **Tracking link** - makes a Campaign on a premade lead form and gives back its link (QR code in All Campaigns). Not on custom forms.
- **Add an unfinished one to Leads** - the same as Add to Leads under Unfinished; a follow-up too, unless they said they have an agent or that follow-up is off.
- **Send an invite to your leads** - the card lists your leads with check boxes (search, Select all shown, a tag like Open house guest or Showcase). People with no email, who unsubscribed or already answered are greyed out. The button says how many get it ("Email 6 people"); it goes from you (your own Gmail or Outlook when connected). Not while RSVPs are closed.
- **Remind** - the card says how many get the reminder; only people emailed from the invite who haven't answered, at most once every 12 hours.
- **Close or reopen RSVPs**, and **copy an invite** (same design and details, new link, no guests).
- Deleting a form, a response, an invite or a campaign stays on the page.
