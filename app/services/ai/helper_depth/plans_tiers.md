Plans in depth: the five plans, exactly which features and sub-options each one gets, AI allowance and storage, trials and the IDX setup fee, broker authorization, what changes on upgrade, downgrade and cancel, and what the No-MLS plans get instead of MLS features.

## The five plans at a glance
- **No-MLS Basic** - $19.95/mo after a 14-day free trial. For agents outside Bright MLS.
- **No-MLS Premium** - $49.95/mo after a 14-day free trial. "All of No-MLS Basic, plus".
- **Basic** - $24.95/mo after a 14-day free trial. Bright MLS.
- **Premium** - $49.95/mo after a 14-day free trial. "Most Popular". "All of Basic, plus".
- **IDX** - $99.95/mo plus a one-time setup fee (currently $500 - the Order Summary always shows the exact amount). No free trial. Card says "+ Setup Fee · Domain Not Included". "All of Basic, Premium, plus".
- Who can pick what: an agent with a Bright MLS ID can pick any plan. An agent with no Bright MLS ID can only pick the two No-MLS plans ("Your account isn't connected to Bright MLS, so these are your plans").
- Grandfathered agents may still pay older prices (Basic $9.95, Premium $24.95) as long as they stay on that plan. Switching to any other plan uses today's price.

Order in each line: No-MLS Basic / No-MLS Premium / Basic / Premium / IDX.

## AI allowance (the "Monthly AI Usage" meter)
- One shared monthly allowance covers every AI tool together. The agent decides how to spend it.
- Relative size: No-MLS Basic and Basic = 1x. No-MLS Premium and Premium = 2x ("2X Basic's AI Credits"). IDX = 4x ("4X Basic's AI Credits"). It is shown as a percent, never as dollars.
- It resets on the 1st of each month. At 100% you see "You've reached this month's AI limit - it resets on the 1st."
- At 90% Media Lab photo and video pause ("AI Studio photo and video are paused until it resets on the 1st. CMAs, cheat sheets and chat still work").
- What uses it: CMA AI analysis (the biggest everyday user), Cheat Sheet Recap, Polish with AI on your bio, the Blog AI writer and its rewrite tools, Spotlight's automatic setup and Room by Room, Media Lab, pasting reviews to import, and the in-app AI helper. Some map and place lookups your actions trigger also count a little.
- The meter shows in Settings and inside the AI tools before you run them (for example "under 1% of your monthly AI").
- Media Lab also has its own count limits on how many photo edits and videos can be made per day and per month.

## Storage
- Every plan has 2 GB of media storage across the whole app (designs, Media Lab results, blog images, logos, photos you upload). When full: "You've used your 2.0 GB of media storage. Delete some photos, videos, blog images or designs to free space."

## Broker authorization (Bright MLS agents only)
- After sign-up, Bright agents must add OpenHousePal as a vendor in Bright MLS before the account switches on. The page "Add OpenHousePal as a vendor in Bright MLS" walks through it: sign in to Bright MLS → your name → Account & Settings → My Subscription → IDX and VOW Licensing Request → add OpenHousePal (about 2 minutes).
- Tap **I've sent the request**. Bright MLS usually approves within 24-48 hours, and OpenHousePal emails the agent. **Check if I'm approved** re-checks.
- While waiting, only **Broker Authorization** shows in the menu. The "About you" setup steps can be done early ("Optional: get a head start").
- Agents who sign up as non-Bright skip this step.
- Showcase, CMA, Discovery and Manager actions refuse with "Broker authorization required" until it's approved.

## Checkout, codes and managed plans
- Pick a plan on Finalize Account (Select Basic / Select Premium / Select IDX / Select No-MLS Basic / Select No-MLS Premium). The Order Summary shows Setup Fee, Free Trial and price after the trial. Pay with PayPal. **Change Selected Plan** goes back.
- **Have a team or promo code?** → **Apply**. Some codes switch the account on with no PayPal at all (button **Switch on my account**). Some codes waive the IDX setup fee.
- Plans paid by OpenHousePal, a team lead or a brokerage show as **Managed Plan** / **Brokerage Paid** - nothing to manage in PayPal; billing questions go to support.

## Upgrading
- Settings → Billing → Plan Options ("Upgrade or Modify") → **Switch to [plan]** → read the window → **Continue to PayPal**. Or tap any locked menu item → **Unlock [feature] - Upgrade Now**.
- New features switch on as soon as PayPal approves (it can take a minute to show).
- Still in the free trial? You keep the trial; the new price starts when it ends. Otherwise the new price starts with your next bill.
- Moving to IDX the first time adds the one-time setup fee, charged once on approval; the monthly IDX price starts on the date you're already paid up to. Enter a code in **Have a code?** to waive it. The fee is only ever paid once - coming back to IDX later doesn't charge it again.
- Moving from Basic up to Premium or IDX: guests without an agent who signed in at your open houses in the last 90 days, and never got a Showcase, now get one built automatically, and their Showcase invite emails go out.

## Downgrading
- The window lists exactly what stops "as soon as the switch takes effect", split into "Your clients will notice" and "You lose access to". Possible items:
  - Your client Showcases pause (they stop getting new homes and price-drop alerts; links show "paused"; new guests don't get one).
  - Your IDX website and Spotlight sites go offline (including links on your own domain).
  - Your Agent Hub page goes offline (links and QR codes, even on printed Brag Sheets and Cheat Sheets, show "not active").
  - Your email signature links stop working.
  - Studio, Blogs and Review Hub are locked (designs, posts and reviews are kept).
  - Media Lab is locked (download what you need first; files are kept but locked).
  - Bright MLS data is removed (moving to a No-MLS plan): Discovery, Manager, CMA, Similar Homes and MLS search go; homes must be typed in.
- "Your data stays in your account. Upgrade again any time and your Showcases, pages, forms and links come right back exactly as they were."

## Cancelling and lapses
- Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription → Need to pause? → **Cancel Plan** → **Yes, Cancel Plan**. Full access continues until the paid period ends; Billing shows **Subscription Ending Soon**.
- On cancel the list also includes: open house sign-in QR codes and kiosks stop accepting guests (even printed ones), forms stop taking responses, calendar and CRM sync stop, and you lose your dashboard and leads.
- After access ends, data may be permanently deleted unless you renew. Before the end date, **Restore Access** reactivates.
- Payment Failed: **Update payment method on PayPal**, or **Start Fresh** (cancels the failed agreement so you can pick a plan with a different card). Expired or Cancelled: **Resubscribe**.
- When a plan lapses, public pages (Agent Hub, forms, Showcases, signature links, reviews, website) stop serving.

## Can't do / common mistakes
- No-MLS agents can't buy Basic, Premium or IDX without a Bright MLS ID on the account (support changes MLS IDs).
- Premium does not include Signature, Media Lab, Listing Spotlight or the IDX website - those need IDX.
- Basic does not include Agent Hub, Showcases, Studio, Blogs or Reviews.
- No plan includes a custom domain purchase in the price; a domain bought in the app renews yearly at its own price.
- The limits agents usually run into are the monthly AI allowance, 2 GB storage, Media Lab counts, 15 served towns, 5 comps per CMA and 100 homes per Showcase.
- Once a day Basic and Premium agents may see "You're missing out on features locked in your nav bar - tap a locked item to see what upgrading unlocks."
