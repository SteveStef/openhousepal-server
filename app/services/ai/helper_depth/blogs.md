Blogs in depth: starting an article, every AI writer question and option, the editor's tabs and sections, looks (and the Classic/Luxe naming), AI cover, fact-check, publishing and scheduling, Google and share settings, and where articles show.

## Who gets it
- Premium, IDX and No-MLS Premium. Locked on Basic and No-MLS Basic.
- **Luxe** article look: IDX and No-MLS Premium.
- **Show on my IDX Site**: IDX only ("Upgrade to IDX to publish your blogs on your own IDX website too").
- What the AI writer draws on depends on your plan: MLS plans - "Your sales, live Bright MLS numbers and verified research on your towns"; No-MLS Premium - "What you told us, plus verified research on the towns you picked".

## Naming you must get right
The looks are **Classic**, **Luxe**, **Editorial**, **Modern**, **Midnight**:
- **Classic** - "Ivory and gold with serif headlines - the standard look" (the default).
- **Luxe** - "Cinematic cover, gold light and motion - the full OpenHousePal Luxe" (IDX / No-MLS Premium), with a **Luxe finish** metal.
- **Editorial** - black-and-white magazine page, big serif type.
- **Modern** - clean, crisp sans-serif with a gold accent.
- **Midnight** - dark and dramatic with warm gold highlights.
Classic has a **Classic color** picker for its accent.

## The Blogs page (Tools > Blogs)
- **Create a Blog** / **Create**. Search ("Search articles..."), status filter, and **Reads** per article.
- Each card: status (Draft, Scheduled, Published, Unpublished, Archived), **Share** (copy link), **Shows on** switches **Agent Hub** and **IDX Site**, delete ("Delete this article?").
- AI drafts in progress show "AI is writing"; failed ones show "AI couldn't finish" / "Unfinished AI draft" with **Try again** / **Finish with AI**.

## Starting an article ("New Article")
1. **What's it about?** - **My own idea** ("Type any topic - a neighborhood, a market question, a story from a closing") or tap an idea. Then **Give it a title** ("A clear, specific title works best - the AI builds the whole article around it").
2. **How should we write it?**
   - **Write it with AI** - "A few quick questions about the area and your take, then a complete article in your voice." Uses your monthly AI allowance.
   - **Start from scratch** - opens with a suggested structure for that kind of article, or a blank article - you write it.

## The AI writer, question by question
- **What angle should it take?** / **What's this article about?** - a sentence is plenty; tap an angle built from your title.
- **What should a reader walk away knowing - or doing?** (optional, sharpens the article).
- **Who is it for?** (pick any): Buyers, Sellers, First-time buyers, Move-up buyers, Luxury buyers, Investors, Relocating, Homeowners.
- **What area are you writing about?** - up to 5 towns or neighborhoods. "Naming them is what makes it rank locally." A switch adds a Town Spotlight for each (a photo, live market numbers, schools and what's nearby - verified and dated). A town not covered yet takes a minute or two the first time.
- **Key points to cover** - bullets are fine.
- **Your take, or a local story only you could tell** - "what makes it yours instead of generic".
- **Numbers you want cited** - "only what you know is true. The AI never invents a statistic."
- **Reference your real sales or listings?** (optional) - pick up to 4 of **Your sales**; **Your active listings** are added as property cards.
- **Tie in one of your videos?** (optional) - an Agent Hub video or a pasted YouTube link; **Where it goes**: **Its own section** (the AI writes about it), **After the intro**, or **Near the end**.
- **Tone**: **Warm & personal** (trusted neighbor), **Professional** (clear, authoritative), **Refined luxury** (editorial, understated), **Straight talk** (plain, zero fluff), **Energetic** (upbeat).
- **Length**: **Quick read** (~650 words, 3 min), **Standard** (~1,000 words, 5 min), **In-depth guide** (~1,700 words, 8 min).
- **What should readers do at the end?** - the closing button opens a form right on the article: Ready to Buy or Sell?, What's My Home Worth?, Schedule a Showing, I'm Buying, I'm Selling, Make Me Sell, or Ask Me a Question.
- Then **Write my article**.

## While it writes
- Stages: "Gathering what we know about your towns", "Researching what is missing" (official sources only - county, school districts, Freddie Mac, the state), "Writing in your voice", "Fact-checking every claim" (numbers, dates, geography, Fair Housing, anything about your experience), "Fixing and finishing".
- "Usually 2-4 minutes." **Keep working - tell me when it's done**: it keeps writing, your bell lights up, and it waits in your Blogs list.
- "A try that fails is never charged." Your answers are saved as a draft.
- If the month's allowance is used: "You've used this month's AI allowance."
- After: **Pick its look** (tap a style on your real article), then **Finish your article** (optional): add a video (**Right after the intro** / **Just before the ending**), **End with a pitch** - "Readers finish with your form", **Show your work above it** (Recent Sales, My listings, Videos about the area - up to 6), and **End with my email signature** (IDX / No-MLS Premium). "Saved as a draft - nothing is published until you say so."
- Existing articles: **Draft with AI**, **Rewrite with AI** ("The AI writer will replace this article's current text with a new draft"), or **Finish with AI** for an unfinished one.

## The editor - 4 tabs
1. **Write** - title, subtitle and sections. **Add section** (or type "/"): *Text* - Heading, Subheading, Paragraph, Quote, Bulleted List, Numbered List, Callout Box, Divider; *Media* - Image, Image Gallery, YouTube Video; *Real Estate* - Property Card, Town Spotlight (from your IDX Areas towns), Agent Profile, QR Code; *Action* - Button, Call to Action, Contact Form (questions arrive in Leads), Lead Form (one of your forms), Related Articles, Disclaimer. Text tools: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, link, text size. Fold, hide (and **Add back**) or **Delete for good** any section.
   - **Refine this section with AI**: **Rewrite it fresher**, **Expand with more depth**, **Make it shorter**, **Make it more local**, **Make it more personal**, **Simplify the language**, or type your own instruction. "Uses your monthly AI credits. Never adds new numbers."
2. **Look & cover** - **Add a cover photo** (a wide JPG; it leads the article, your Hub card and every share; JPG under 10MB), **Category** (Buyers, Sellers, Market Updates, Neighborhoods, Homeownership, Luxury, Investments, Open Houses, Local Guides, New Construction, Real Estate News), **Look** (the 5 looks), **Color** / **Finish**.
   - **AI cover**: the AI suggests a picture; describe or tweak it ("make it dusk, lose the snow") -> **Make the change** -> **Use as cover**. "Usually 20-40 seconds." Uses AI allowance; "Couldn't make that picture - nothing was charged."
3. **Fact-check & finish** - the **Verified** report: scores (Accuracy, Local detail, Usefulness, Sounds like you, Readability, Search, Compliance), **Still needs your eye** (flags such as Unsupported claim, Wrong number, Out-of-date number, Geography, Fair Housing, School wording, Your experience, Legal / tax), **Numbers to double-check**, **Where the facts came from**, **Make it even stronger**.
4. **Review and Publish** - a checklist (**A real title**, **Cover photo**, **Category**, **Search description**, **Share image**) and every setting:
   - **Tags**, **Featured on Agent Hub** (pins it to the front of your Hub's articles).
   - **Article link** - the end of the address (/blogs/...). Changing it on a live article "breaks links people already shared and resets where it shows in Google" - **Change the link** / **Keep the old link**.
   - **On Google**: **Search title**, **Search description**, **Let Google find this article** (off = hidden from search, still works by link), with a Google preview.
   - **When it's shared**: **Share image** (otherwise the cover), **Share title**, **Share description**.
   - **Neighborhood pages** - link the towns to their pages on your IDX website.
   - **Where it shows**: **Show on my Agent Hub** (Blogs section), **Show on my IDX Site** (IDX: Journal and Latest Articles). "Hiding keeps it published - just off that page."
   - **End with my email signature** - your signature card replaces the small contact card.

## Publishing and scheduling
- **Publish now**, or **Or schedule it**: quick picks **Tomorrow 8 AM**, **This Saturday 9 AM**, **Next Monday 8 AM**, or your own date and time ("Goes live ...").
- Live articles: **Update published article**, **View live**, **Copy Link**. You can unpublish it (status Unpublished) and publish it again later.
- Autosaves (Saving... / Saved / Save failed). **Preview** any time.

## Can't do / common mistakes
- More than 5 towns or 4 sales in one AI article isn't possible.
- The AI never makes up statistics - give it your numbers in "Numbers you want cited".
- Town Spotlight needs towns set up in IDX > Areas.
- "Classic" is the standard look; "Luxe" is the premium one - don't mix them up.
- Basic, Premium and No-MLS plans can't show articles on an IDX site.
- Read the fact-check flags before publishing; you're responsible for what goes live.
