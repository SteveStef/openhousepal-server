# SHOWCASES

## Who gets it
- Premium and IDX. Basic sees it locked with an upgrade prompt. Hidden on No-MLS plans. Broker authorization must be finished.

## Where Showcases come from
- Open house guests without an agent (kit's **Guest Showcases** on, or the per-visitor switch).
- A Similar Homes sheet QR ("See more like this?"), the **Setup My Own Showcase** form (Brag Sheet QR or its link), the I'm Buying and How Can We Help forms (built quietly for buyers without an agent), the Guest Pass **Create my Showcase** button, and your IDX website (**Create my free Showcase** / **Save this search**).
- By hand: Showcases → **Create**. Sort by **Came from** to see the source.

## Creating one for a client
1. **About them**: Client's full name, Email, Phone (optional), **Showcase name** ("Named from your client - change it to anything"). **Email the client their Showcase** sends their link, what it looks for and a few homes when you create it.
2. **Why is the client looking?** (Looking to buy soon, Just browsing, Interested in area, Investment opportunity, Curious about property) and **Does the client have an agent?** (Yes, they have an agent / No, they don't have an agent / They're looking for an agent).
3. **Set the search area** (the map window "Where should we look"), then **Create Showcase**. The client is added to your Leads.

## Every search option
- **Price**: Min price / Max price (No limit).
- **Bedrooms & baths**: Bedrooms - at least, Baths - at least, Max beds, Max baths.
- **Size, lot & age**: Min/Max sq ft, Min/Max acres, Built after, Built before.
- **Where** (how they combine: towns and drawn shapes add together; a county with towns keeps only the towns inside it (towns outside it match nothing - the editor says so); a search center's radius is its own area when no town or county is picked - with towns or a county picked it narrows them: only homes in those places AND inside the circle (the editor says so, with **Remove circle** to show the whole town, and turns red with tips when nothing is inside both). Adding a town while there's a circle: a town completely outside the circle takes the circle off on its own ("Search circle removed - Exton is outside it", with **Undo**); a town the circle only partly covers opens a pop-up "The circle only covers part of Exton" (how many of its homes are inside) with **Remove the circle**, **Grow the circle to N mi** (same center, takes in the whole town), **Move the center to Exton** (keeps the size), **Pick a new center on the map** (map editor only) or **Keep it as is** - same in the Edit Showcase pop-up; a drawn shape and a radius add together, each exact): **Towns** ("Search towns..."), **Counties**, **School districts** ("narrows every area"), **Search center** (an address or a dropped pin, with a radius - press and drag out from a point to set it), **Draw a search area** (trace with finger or mouse; **Add another shape**; **Clear all**).
- **Property type**: Single Family, Condo, Townhouse, Rentals, Multi-Family, Lot/Land, Commercial, Farm.
- **Must-haves (AI)** (optional, MLS plans): up to 3 things like "First-floor primary bedroom", "9 ft+ ceilings", "Pool" (type one or tap a suggestion). The AI reads every home the search finds; homes that have **all** of them get the Recommended star, as if you starred it, and a gold line under the address ("This appears to be on a cul-de-sac") - the client sees it too. When saved, each must-have is tidied once: spelling fixed ("col de sack" becomes "Cul-de-sac") and the words for the card line and follow-up text worked out. Homes that don't still show, without the star. One-level homes count for anything on the first floor; numbers mean at least (a 4-car garage counts for 2-car). Each home is read once; new listings are read as they arrive. It uses a little of their chat usage; with none left it pauses ("Paused - not enough chat usage left") until the billing day. Take a star off and it stays off. Change the must-haves and every home is read again; remove them all to turn it off (stars it gave stay). It's the last section of the search editor on purpose - set the search first, then the must-haves: the box shows how many homes the AI will read and about how much chat usage that takes ("Reads 88 homes · uses ~2% of chat usage · 61% left"), like the chat's Home search card. Same limits as the chat: 300 homes at most (a bigger search can't add must-haves - narrow it first; one that grows past 300 pauses: "Paused - 340 homes to read…"), and only when the chat usage has room. The Must-haves bar on the Showcase shows how much chat usage its reading has used. Open one of those homes and the listing's own words the AI found are highlighted gold in the description, with a "Highlighted below:" line naming the must-have (tap it to jump to the words) - for the agent and the client; take the must-haves off and the highlight goes too. A client's follow-up text also says how many of their Showcase homes have them, right after the home count ("4 of them are on a cul-de-sac."; if the tidy-up ever failed: "4 of them match what you asked for: cul-de-sac."), and the home it names first is one of those (even one listed more than 3 weeks ago - then without the "hit the market" line) - nothing to edit in the follow-up settings, it's added only when must-haves are on. With must-haves on, an open house guest's text calls them "homes that might interest you", never "similar to the one you toured". The Showcase link's preview picture (the card in iMessage) leads with the same homes: the one the text names first, then the other must-have homes, then the newest; it changes as soon as the Showcase's homes change.
- **Match score**: "Showing the % Match badge" or "% Match badge hidden".
- **Comparing to**: **Set compare property** / **Change compare property** - the home every match is scored against (e.g. the open house home).
- A live list updates as you go ("Updating matches…"). You must set at least one area, shape or search center.

## Match score (% Match)
- How similar each home is to the compare home: distance, same school district/town, size, home type, lot, age and style, and how recent. Condition and renovations are not part of it. Missing facts are left out rather than scored as zero.

## The 100-home limit
- A Showcase holds the best 100 for-sale homes (closest first for a distance search, newest otherwise). Over that it shows "100+ homes match your search" with a tip to tighten price, beds, towns or area. Homes the client liked, commented on or toured, and your own picks, are always kept on top.

## The Showcases page
- Search "Search visitor, property, location...", **Tours** and **Activity** buttons, **Budgets** filter, sort (Activity, New matches, Newest, Fell asleep, Came from), **Active** / **Inactive** tabs, **New matches** filter.
- Card: star, copy client email/phone, new-match count (**Dismiss new matches**), activity badges, **Edit**, **Share**, **Delete**, the invite button, **On / Off**.
- Invite button on each card: a small **Email** button once the client got their Showcase invite email (tap = **Send invite again**); an amber **Send invite** (just **Invite** on a narrow phone) when they never got one - it was never sent, held back by the email settings, or didn't go through. Either asks first ("We'll email ... their Showcase link with the N homes in it, from you, right now" -> **Send invite** / **Not now**). It goes even if the same email went out that day. Not offered when the Showcase is asleep, the client unsubscribed, or Showcase emails are off for them.

## Inside one Showcase
- With must-haves on, a **Must-haves** bar under the tabs shows them and how many homes have them all ("Reading 40 homes for these…" while it reads); **Change** opens the search editor.
- Tabs **Matches**, **Liked**, **Disliked**, **Sold/Removed**. Sort: Best Match, Days on Market, Last Updated, Price per Sq Ft, Square Feet, Lot Size (Acres), Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Year Built (low/high).
- **Comparing to** / **Searching around** bar - tap to change the compare home or how far out it searches.
- **Share** (copies the link; asks to "Turn on the link and share it" if it's off), **Edit Search**, **View tours**, **Turn Off / Turn On**.
- Email switches: **Emails to the client** and **Emails to me** (shown as Visitor / Agent on a computer). "This visitor unsubscribed from update emails" if they did.
- **Select** mode: **Recommend** ("I recommend this home for you" tag; brings a passed home back), **Love** / **Pass** (recorded as if the client did it), **To Sold/Removed** (off-market homes only), **Remove** (the search won't add it back), **Send**.
- A home's details window (tap the card) has the same tools as its card, so you never close it to act: **Recommend** with a small **Love** / **Pass** beside it, **Send** (the Send window opens over it; green **Sent** once sent), **Tours** when someone asked to tour it, and **Directions**. On a phone, once you scroll, the bottom bar has Recommend, Love, Pass, Send and Messages.
- **Tidy up** (beside the Showcase's name, only when homes sold or came off the market): moves every sold / under contract / off-market home into Sold/Removed in one go - except ones the client loved, commented on or asked to tour, which stay for you to move by hand with the card's **Move to Sold/Removed**. It shows how many move and how many stay before anything happens.
- **Send**: by Text (opens Messages) or Email (pick Gmail, Outlook or your default mail app), with the home links into their Showcase. **Attach a short message?** - **Save as my message** saves it for every Showcase. The Showcase link must be on first.
- **Mention the must-haves?** (in Send, only when a home being sent has every must-have; on by default): adds a line after the message - one home: "This one is on a cul-de-sac."; several that all have them: "All 3 of these homes are on a cul-de-sac."; some of several: "2 of these 3 homes are on a cul-de-sac: 12 Oak St and 22 Birch Ln." Untick it to leave it out. Works for texts and emails, from the Showcase page and the chat Showcase card.
- Add one home from any home's page: **Add to a Showcase** → pick the client. It's marked Recommended, stays even if it doesn't match, and counts as new for them.

## Changing the search
- **Edit** → change anything → **OK, save search**. The client hears about it in that afternoon's email (what it looks for now, homes added or gone).
- If the client asked ("the client asked you to refine their search"), tap **Edit Search** or dismiss it. Clients can also change their own search; that shows in your briefing.

## Client emails and digests
- Showcase invite after your open house: 1 hour after sign-in by default (Right away, 15, 30 minutes or 1 hour in Tools → Email).
- Did a client get their invite? The lead's page (Their Showcase card) says **Invite emailed** + date, or **Invite never sent** / **Invite not sent - held back by your email settings** / **Invite didn't go through**, with **Send invite** or **Send invite again**. When it was opened is in that lead's **Emails** card.
- **New homes & price drops**: at most one email a day at 4:30 PM Eastern, only on days something changed. Or **Once a week** for everyone (Tools → Email → How often: Up to daily / Once a week). Each person can also be slowed automatically.
- Every row in Tools → Email can be switched off and sent **From your email** (connected Gmail) or **OpenHousePal**.
- Automatic slowing: unopened on 3 different days → weekly; 3 ignored weekly emails → automatic emails stop. Opening one or using the Showcase turns daily back on.

## Sleep and wake
- Falls asleep (moves to Inactive) when automatic emails have stopped for that client and you haven't touched them in 2 weeks (opened the Showcase or lead, marked contacted, wrote a note, checked off a follow-up). A client still using their Showcase stays awake. Asleep = no new matches, emails or alerts; nothing deleted; link still works.
- Starred Showcases never fall asleep. Starring one (or the lead) wakes it.
- Wakes by itself when the client opens their Showcase or an old email, and catches up on homes listed meanwhile. You can wake it with **Turn On** / **On**.
- **Turn Off** = "Put to sleep: no new matches, no emails" by hand.

## Tours, comments and likes
- **Tours**: **Confirm** → pick the time → **Yes, Confirm Tour**. Only confirm once it's booked in ShowingTime and the listing agent accepted - the client gets a confirmation email and it's added to your connected calendar. **Unconfirm** reverses. **Mark Done** clears a request without telling the client; **Reopen** undoes.
- **Comments**: clients comment on homes; you get an alert follow-up. **Reply to Comment** - your reply is emailed to the client.
- **Likes** (the client's favorites): show in the Liked tab; you get one "Liked homes" email per hour of activity and a next-morning follow-up naming the homes.
- What the client sees: Matches / Liked / Disliked / Sold/Removed, a heart, pass, comments, tour requests, **Change your search**, an **Emails** switch, and "Your Upcoming Tours". When you open their link: "Your clicks here don't count as theirs."

## The Showcase card in the chat
- Whenever the chat reads a client's Showcase, its homes show as a card under the reply instead of a text list: each home's photo, street, price, beds/baths, Sold / Under Contract, what the client did (Loved, Passed, Commented, Tour ask, Sent) and, after a search, a short piece of the listing's words with the key part highlighted. **Recommend** (star), **Send** (paper plane) and **Remove** (trash can, asks first) are round buttons in each home's top right corner; a long street name ends in "...". Matches the chat thinks aren't real (a pool table, a community pool) sit last in Matches, greyed, marked "Maybe not" with why.
- On every home: **Recommend** (the star; shows at the top of their Showcase), **Send** (the same text / email window as the Showcase page, opened over the chat - the chat stays where it was) and the trash can (**Remove** - asks "for good?" first; the search won't add it back).
- **Select several**: check homes, then Send, Recommend or Remove them together (Remove asks first).
- Big Showcases: chips (Matches, All, Recommended, Loved / commented, Off the market), a search box, and 8 homes at a time with "Show more". **Open <name>'s Showcase** goes to the page.
- Opened again later, the card shows the Showcase as it is now.

## Checking every home for one thing (in the helper chat)
- Ask "which homes in <lead>'s Showcase have a first-floor bedroom" (or a pool, a finished basement, a garage...) and AI reads every home in the Showcase (up to 300) - the listing description, the MLS feature lists and the MLS bedroom-by-floor counts ("Entry Level Bedroom", main-level bedrooms) - so any wording counts ("1st floor", "owner's suite on the main level"). A one-level home counts for anything on the first floor, and numbers mean at least ("a 2-car garage" includes 3- and 4-car garages; "9-foot ceilings" includes 10-foot). The matches show first on the Showcase card with the listing's own words; homes that only hint at it (a community pool, high ceilings with no height given) show greyed as "Maybe not".
- It uses a little chat usage (about half a percent for 100 homes on a Basic plan), checked before it starts: without enough left it says so and shows simple word matches instead.
- A home that isn't a match didn't mention it - it may still have it. Nothing in the Showcase changes.
- Then "remove the ones that don't have it": the chat makes a **Remove homes from <name>'s Showcase** card - every home in the Showcase with its photo, price and beds, the ones to go already checked (red). Homes the client loved, commented on or asked to tour start unchecked; check them to remove too. Search, quick filters (All / Checked / Loved or commented / Off the market) and Check all shown. Nothing leaves until **Remove N homes**; removed homes leave for good (the search won't add them back), same as Select mode's Remove.

## Can't do
- **Delete Showcase** is permanent (homes, likes, comments, search; the client stays in Leads; open follow-ups about it may be removed - the window lists them). Use **Turn Off** to pause.
- Turning a kit's Guest Showcases on doesn't build them for guests who already signed in - use the Visitors window's Showcase switch for those.
- **Edit Showcase** (a button on a lead's Showcase card, and under each guest's Showcase switch on an open house's Visitors list): a quick pop-up with towns, school district, price, beds, baths, sq ft and built-in-or-after, starting from what the Showcase has now and showing how many homes match as you go. Remove takes off the open house's distance circle; Start from scratch clears the open house's starting search; a max price under the starting min clears that min. Two Showcases = pick which. If the client asked you to change their search, their message shows at the top and saving marks it done (and checks off its follow-up). An "Also in this search" line lists what the window doesn't edit (home types, counties, max beds/baths, acres, a drawn area). A waiting invite email is updated to the new home count. Saving refreshes the homes ("Updated - 8 homes (3 new · 15 gone)") and their follow-up message shows the new count and link - and once the open house circle is removed, it says the Showcase is "based on what you told me you're looking for" instead of "similar to the one you toured" (fixed wording, not an editable situation).
- Turning a guest's Showcase on says when the invite email goes out (about an hour later, unless the invite email is off in Tools → Email); switching it off before then cancels it.
- Picking towns, townships or counties makes the search those places only - a radius from the starting home never adds homes from nearby towns. If the Showcase still has a radius, it narrows the towns: only homes in the towns that are also inside the circle (Edit Showcase says so; tap **Remove** on the distance to show the whole town). A guest's open house Showcase starts as a circle around the open house (no towns); adding a town outside that circle removes the circle on its own, and a town it partly covers asks remove / grow / move the center. A school district narrows whatever area is picked.
- No Showcases on No-MLS plans (no MLS homes to match).

# CMA (Tools → CMA, "Price it right, prove it")

## Who gets it
- Basic, Premium, IDX with broker authorization. Hidden on No-MLS plans.

## From the AI helper chat
- "Start a CMA for ..." shows a card with two steps: **Who it's for** (Seller / Listing or Buyer / Offer), then two choices: **AI CMA - I do all of it** (picks the comps, runs the AI analysis and writes the report; it shows about how much of the monthly AI it uses) or **Manual** (makes the CMA and they pick the comps on the CMA page - no AI). An AI CMA takes a few minutes (it picks the comps, then the analysis and report - all in the background, the card answers right away); they can close the chat or the site. When it's done, the chat says it's finished with an **Open the CMA** button, and the AI helper button shows a red number until they open that chat - on any device.
- A Showcase made or changed from the chat can use **Built in or after** a year too ("newer builds" with no year = about the last 15 years), along with price, beds, baths, size, towns, townships, school districts and home types. A phone number for the client is optional.

## The list
- Search "Search by address or client...", tabs All, Draft, Ready, Shared. Each card: Fair Value, **Copy Share Link**, **Download PDF** / **Save the PDF**, theme (light or **Midnight**), **Delete** (permanent).

## Steps (one CMA can be left and picked up later; it saves as you go)
1. **Create New CMA**: optional Client name, then **This CMA is for a...** **Seller / Listing** ("What should this home list for?") or **Buyer / Offer** ("What should my buyer offer?").
2. **Select the Subject Property**: **Search Address**, **My Listings**, or **Enter Manually** ("Fill in the home's facts - the more complete they are, the better the value"). Fix any fact that isn't right (sqft above grade, basement, garage, water/sewer, etc.), then **Find comps**. A rental listing can't be the subject.
3. **Find Comparables**: "Top matches auto-selected. Add or remove as you see fit." Up to 5 comps ("A CMA can have at most 5 comparables"). **Refine Your Search**: Status (Active, Active Under Contract, Pending, Sold), **Sold in the last** (90, 180, 365, 730 days, Any time), radius, Min/Max Price, Min Beds, Min Baths, Min/Max Acres, Min/Max Sq Ft, Min/Max Year Built, **School District**. Cards show tags like Same street, Same school, Quiet street / Near a major road. A comp with no sold price on record shows last asking and can't be used in the value. List, map or both.
4. **Analysis**: **Create AI Analysis** - "Reads every comp's photos and data, rates strength and quality, and proposes a value estimate." Takes a few minutes; you can watch progress. It shows how much of your monthly AI allowance it uses before and after. On each comp: rate it against the subject (Comp Inferior / Similar / Comp Superior on Finish, Interior and Exterior Quality and more), **Comp Weight**, **Your note on this comp**, **Mark Irrelevant** (with a reason). After changing comps, recompute the valuation from the current set.
5. **Pricing** ("Pricing Conclusion" - "Auto-filled by AI once generated - your numbers always override it"):
   - Seller: Recommended Min, Recommended Max, Most Likely Market Value, **Recommended List Price**, Agent Pricing Analysis, **Pricing Strategy**, **Listing Prep**.
   - Buyer: Estimated Fair Market Value, Suggested Offer Min/Max, **Strong Offer**, Buyer Valuation - Agent Notes, **Inspection Concerns**, **Offer Suggestion**.
6. **Review** ("Final Review"): Estimated Fair Value, Recommended Value Range, Agent Conclusion, "How we got there". **Presentation**: hide any section clients shouldn't see - Subject Property, Comparable Overview, Location Context, Individual Comparables, Market Context, Market Analysis, Current Competition (today's competing listings), Agent Final Analysis. Saved changes update the live link immediately.
7. **Complete & Send**: **Mark Ready**, **Open PDF** / **Save the PDF**, **Share link** (copy or send), **My Signature** (your signature card closes the web version; the PDF keeps its own footer), **Visitor Theme** (Ivory or Midnight).

## Good to know
- The share link always shows the latest saved version - no need to re-share. There's no "unshare".
- Each CMA has a **Report Code**; if numbers or wording look wrong, **Submit Ticket** with it.
- A final consistency check runs before export; if a section no longer matches the final value, the report may need checking again before it exports.
- The per-comp adjustment table ("How we got there") is for the agent only - the client PDF shows the steps from the adjusted sales to the value, not the table.
- Auction listings (MLS Sale Type "Auction", or "opening bid" in the listing): the list price is treated as the opening bid, not as value evidence; the value comes from the sales, and the buyer advice is a bidding plan (top bid = the value) instead of an offer below list.
- Out of AI allowance: the analysis won't run until it resets on the 1st; you can still build comps and price by hand.
- Old Seller Net Sheet / Buyer Cash Needed links open the CMA page.
