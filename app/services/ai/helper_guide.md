# The OpenHousePal guide

Everything below is how OpenHousePal works today, written for agents. Button and menu names are exactly as they appear on screen.


## OpenHousePal guide: menus, plans, your website, Agent Hub, Settings and setup

This guide is for real estate agents using OpenHousePal. It covers where everything lives, what each plan includes, your IDX website, your Agent Hub, your Settings, and the Account setup walkthrough. Button and menu names are written exactly as they appear on screen.

---

### 1. Where things are

### On a computer (the bar across the top)

From left to right, after the OpenHousePal logo:

| Menu | Where it goes | What's under it (hover to open) |
|---|---|---|
| **Dash** | Your dashboard | - |
| **Opens** | Your open houses | **My Opens**, **Discovery**, **Manager** |
| **Showcases** | Your client Showcases | - |
| **Leads** | Your leads | **Leads**, **Email Tracker**, **Follow-ups**, **Activity**, and **Action Plans** (marked "Soon", not available yet) |
| **Tools** | The Tools page, which lists every tool | **Agent Hub**, **Forms & Invites**, **CMA**, **Blogs**, **Studio**, **Media Lab**, **Listing Spotlight**, **Signature**, **CRM**, **Email**, **Calendar**, **Reviews**, and **Deadlines** (marked "Soon", not available yet) |
| **IDX** | Your IDX website editor | - |

On the right, after a thin divider, there are small icons with no words. Hover over one to see its name:

- **Account setup** (clipboard icon): reopens the step-by-step setup.
- **Help** (question mark): your support tickets. A red dot means support has replied.
- **Settings** (gear): your profile, notifications, billing and account. A red "!" means something required is still missing.
- The **bell**: your notifications.
- **Sun / moon**: switches the app between light and dark.
- **Logout**.

### On a phone

Tap the menu button at the top right. Every section is a card. Tap a section to unfold its pages:

- **Dashboard**
- **Open Houses**: My Opens, Discovery, Manager
- **Showcases**
- **Leads**: Leads, Email Tracker, Follow-ups, Activity (Action Plans shows "Coming soon")
- **Tools**: **Branding**, plus the same tools as on a computer (Agent Hub, Forms & Invites, CMA, Blogs, Studio, Media Lab, Listing Spotlight, Signature, CRM, Email, Calendar, Reviews). Deadlines shows "Coming soon".
- **IDX Website**
- **Account**: **Account setup**, **Settings**, **Help**

The bell and the light/dark switch sit next to the menu button.

### Why some menu items look different

- **A lock icon, greyed out**: your plan doesn't include it. Tap it to see which plan unlocks it.
- **Missing entirely**: the feature needs Bright MLS data, and your plan is one of the No-MLS plans. For example, No-MLS plans have no Discovery, Manager, CMA, Showcases or IDX.
- **Only "Broker Authorization" shows**: your broker hasn't approved your account yet.
- **Everything is faded and can't be tapped**: your subscription has ended. Go to **Settings → Billing** to come back.
- **Menu items take you to checkout**: you haven't picked a plan yet.
- Once a day, Basic and Premium agents may see a reminder: "You're missing out on features locked in your nav bar — tap a locked item to see what upgrading unlocks."

### 2. Plans and billing

### The five plans

There are two families of plans. Bright MLS agents can choose any plan. Agents outside Bright MLS can only choose the two No-MLS plans. At checkout they see the message "Your account isn't connected to Bright MLS, so these are your plans."

**Bright MLS plans**

- **Basic**: **$24.95/mo** after a 14-day free trial. Includes:
  - Open House Kits & Sign-In: QR or kiosk sign-in, a Brag Sheet of your recent sales, a Similar Homes flyer and a Cheat Sheet.
  - Forms & Invites.
  - Leads & Follow-Ups.
  - CMA Builder.
  - Discovery + Manager: find open houses to host, and approve hosts on your own listings.
  - Calendar & CRM Sync.
  - Bright MLS Integration.
- **Premium**: **$49.95/mo** after a 14-day free trial. Marked "Most Popular". All of Basic, plus:
  - **Agent Hub**
  - **Showcases**
  - **Studio** (flyers, mailers and social posts)
  - **Blogs**
  - **Review Hub**
  - 2X Basic's AI Credits
- **IDX**: **$99.95/mo** plus a one-time setup fee. Marked "Full Website". The card says "+ Setup Fee · Domain Not Included". IDX has **no free trial**. All of Basic and Premium, plus:
  - **IDX Website**: a complete website with property search, plus Custom Domain Support.
  - **Media Lab** (AI twilight, staging and photo-to-video)
  - **Listing Spotlight** (a standalone site for each listing)
  - **Signature** (your email signature)
  - 4X Basic's AI Credits

**No-MLS plans** (no Bright MLS required)

- **No-MLS Basic**: **$19.95/mo** after a 14-day free trial. Includes:
  - Open House Sign-In: you type in the home's details. Comes with a Brag Sheet of the sales you add.
  - Forms & Invites.
  - Leads & Follow-Ups.
  - Calendar & CRM Sync.
  - Your Brand, Everywhere (Brand Kit).
  - Send From Your Email.
- **No-MLS Premium**: **$49.95/mo** after a 14-day free trial. All of No-MLS Basic, plus:
  - **Agent Hub** (you add your sales by hand)
  - **Media Lab**
  - **Studio**
  - **Blogs**
  - **Review Hub**
  - **Signature**

### Starting your plan (checkout)

1. After your broker approves you, the **Finalize Account** page shows the plan cards. Bright agents can switch between **Bright MLS Agent** and **Non-Bright MLS**.
2. Tap **Select Basic**, **Select Premium**, **Select IDX**, **Select No-MLS Basic** or **Select No-MLS Premium**.
3. The **Order Summary** shows the Setup Fee, the Free Trial and your price after the trial.
4. Pay securely with PayPal. When it's done, you land on your Dash.
5. Have a code? Use the **Have a team or promo code?** box, type it in, and tap **Apply**. Some codes switch your account on with no PayPal at all. In that case the button is **Switch on my account**, and you're billed directly.
6. Picked the wrong plan? Tap **Change Selected Plan**.

### Seeing your plan

Go to **Settings → Billing**. You can also tap the plan name button at the top of Settings. You'll see:

- **Current Plan** and its price.
- A status: **Free Trial**, **Active**, **Cancelled**, **Payment Failed** or **Expired**.
- Dates: **Trial Ends**, **Active Since**, **Last Billing**, **First Payment** / **Next Renewal**, or **Access Ends** if you've cancelled.
- **View full plan details** opens the full card for your plan.

If your plan is paid by OpenHousePal directly, or by your team lead or brokerage, it shows as **Managed** or **Brokerage Paid**. There's nothing to manage in PayPal. Message support with any billing questions.

### Upgrading or switching plans

1. Go to **Settings → Billing** and find **Plan Options** ("Upgrade or Modify").
2. Tap **Switch to [plan name]**.
3. Read the confirmation. If you're moving to a smaller plan, it lists exactly what stops working. For example, your Agent Hub going offline or your Showcases pausing.
4. Tap **Continue to PayPal** and approve the change there.

What to expect:

- The new plan switches on as soon as PayPal approves it.
- If you're still in your free trial, you keep the trial. The new price starts when the trial ends.
- Otherwise, the new price starts with your next bill.
- **Moving to IDX** shows a **one-time IDX setup fee**. PayPal charges it once when you approve. If you have a code, enter it in the **Have a code?** box to waive the fee.

You can also upgrade from any locked menu item. It opens an **Unlock More** page that compares your current plan with the plan you need. Tap **Unlock [feature] — Upgrade Now**.

### Cancelling

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription**.
2. Under **Need to pause?**, tap **Cancel Plan**.
3. The confirmation (**Cancel Subscription?**) lists what stops. Tap **Yes, Cancel Plan** to confirm.

You keep full access until the end of the period you already paid for. Billing then shows **Subscription Ending Soon** and the number of days left. After access ends, your data may be permanently deleted unless you renew.

### Payment failed or plan ended

- **Payment Failed**:
  - Tap **Update payment method on PayPal** to fix your card.
  - Or tap **Start Fresh** to cancel the failed agreement and pick a plan with a different card.
- **Expired** or **Cancelled**: tap **Resubscribe**.

### 3. Your Agent Hub

**What it is:** one page with everything about you. It has your photo, bio, contact buttons, socials, listings, recent sales, videos, house tours, blogs and reviews. Visitors can tap **Save Contact** to save your details to their phone, or **Schedule a Showing**. If you're on a Bright plan, your listings and sales update themselves from the MLS.

**Plans:** Premium, IDX, No-MLS Premium.

**Where:** **Tools → Agent Hub**. The editor's headline is "One link, all of you". It has six tabs: **Profile**, **Contact**, **Listings & Sales**, **Media**, **Layout** and **Link**. A live preview sits beside the editor. Your view counts (**Views**, **Interacted**) appear at the top.

### Your hub's address

Your hub lives at **openhousepal.com/your-name**. It also has:

- A Sales page at openhousepal.com/your-name/sales, with **Past Sales** and **Current Listings**.
- A listings page.
- A blogs page.
- A review page at openhousepal.com/your-name/review.

If you're on IDX and have connected your own domain, your hub also opens on your domain.

**To change the address:**

1. Open the **Link** tab.
2. Under **Public URL**, type a new **Custom URL ending**. Use letters, numbers and hyphens only.
3. Save. The app asks **Change your Agent Hub link?** - pick a name you'll keep: your current link keeps working after the change (business cards, signature, printed QR codes still open your hub, and no other agent can take it), but only your most recent old link keeps working - change it again later and that one stops. The new link becomes your main one, used everywhere.

**Is it on Google?** Without IDX: no - the Agent Hub, its Sales and Listings pages at openhousepal.com/your-name aren't listed on Google (people open them from your link, QR codes, cards and signature). Blog articles ARE listed (leave **Let Google find this article** on). With IDX: the hub, Sales page and articles are on your own website (your-name.openhousepal.com or your own domain - /hub, /sales, /blog), where Google lists them. An **On Google** card says this in setup (Agent Hub link, Sales), the Agent Hub **Public URL** panel and an article's Publish screen, with **See the IDX plan**. Spotlights (IDX) are always listed.
4. Only change it if you haven't shared your link yet, or you're ready to update it everywhere.

The Link tab also has buttons to copy, open and share your link.

### Profile and Contact

These are the same details you see in **Settings → Profile**. Change them in either place and both update.

- **Profile**: your photo, Full Name, Title, Brokerage, Team, Market and About.
- **Contact**: call, text and email.
- **Social Links**: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and Website.
- **Contact Card**: what people get when they tap Save Contact.

### Listings & Sales

- **My Listings**: choose which listings appear on your hub. Tap **Refresh Listings** to pull in the latest. To add a listing yourself, tap **Manually Add** → **Add Listing** and enter the Street Address, City, State, Price, Status (Active, Coming Soon or Under Contract) and a Photo (JPG, under 5MB).
- **Recent Sales**: choose which sales appear on your hub and rotate through your email signature. Tap **Refresh Sales** to look them up again. To add a sale yourself, tap **Manually Add** → **Add Sold Property**, then enter the Street Address, City, State, Price, Date Closed and Photo.
  - Sales are found using your RE License number. If none show up, add your license number first.
  - Other options: sort order (Newest, Price High, Price Low), **Min price to display**, **Sold-price stats**, **Carousel view** and **Auto scroll**.
  - No-MLS Premium agents add every sale by hand. Those sales also print on the open house Brag Sheet.

### Media

Add YouTube **Videos**, **House Tours** and **Blogs** to your hub.

### Layout

- **Agent Hub theme**: choose **My Brand Kit** to follow your Branding, so your hub changes when your Branding changes. Or choose **Choose my own** and pick a **Classic color** or a **Luxe finish**.
  - Luxe only shows to visitors on the IDX plan. On other plans you can preview it, but visitors see Classic.
  - Changes go live right away.
- **Ivory or Midnight**: a light or dark page for visitors. This also applies to your Sales page.
- **Profile header**: how the top of your hub looks on phones and computers.
- **Reviews**: three switches:
  - **Show reviews on my Agent Hub**
  - **Only show 4 and 5 star reviews** (this also applies to your IDX website)
  - **Move on their own (phones)**
- **Agent Hub Layout**: drag the sections (My Listings, Recent Sales, Videos, House Tours, Blogs, Reviews) into the order visitors see them. Switch each section on or off.
- **Star Rating**: **Star rating on my profile card** and **Review button on my profile card**.

### Where your listings link to

In **Settings → Profile**, the **Where Your Listings Link To** panel shows where each listing on your hub (and in your email signature) opens. It opens either your Listing Spotlight for that home, if you've published one, or the simple one-page listing page. From here you can send a listing back to the plain page.

### The Listing Page

This is the simple one-page view of a single home. It opens from:

- Similar Homes QR codes
- Agent Hub listing cards
- Your email signature
- Guest Passes

It shows "Presented by" you, with a schedule-a-showing form and a message form. Requests from it arrive in your Leads.

### Common questions

- **My hub says "This link isn't active."** The hub only shows while your plan includes it and your subscription is active.
- **Why don't I see Luxe on my live hub?** Luxe is an IDX-plan look.
- **My sales aren't showing.** Add your license number. If it's already saved, tap **Refresh Sales**. You can always use **Manually Add**.

### 4. Your IDX website

**What it is:** a full website with live home search, your listings, sales, neighborhoods (town pages), blog, reviews and contact forms. It's built automatically from what you've already set up.

**Plans:** IDX only.

**Where:** **IDX** in the top menu (on a phone, **IDX Website**). The editor's headline is "Your site, your brand". The status chip shows **Live**, **Draft** or **Turned off**. Next to it are buttons to copy your link or open the site in a new window.

### Editor tabs

- **Sections** ("Build Your Home Page"): drag sections into order, switch them on or off, or tap one to edit it. Add more from the catalogue. Available sections include:
  - Welcome Screen
  - Featured Homes
  - Just Listed
  - Listing Spotlights
  - Open Houses
  - Recently Sold
  - Showcase Collection
  - Neighborhoods
  - About Me
  - Client Reviews
  - Lead Form
  - Contact Band
  - Latest Articles
  - Text, Photo, Divider and Heading
- **Design** ("Look & Feel"): choose between:
  - **Classic**: "Clean and bright... great for most agents".
  - **Luxe**: "Cinematic: deep panels, metallic accents, gliding photo strips. For high-end brands".
  - You can also set an accent color, **Ivory** or **Midnight**, fonts, your logo and **Let visitors switch** (a sun/moon button for visitors). Pick **My Brand Kit** to follow your Branding, or **Choose my own**.
- **Featured** ("Featured Homes"): the homes at the top of your home page (Towns, Price, Home type, **My own listings first**) and what your Featured page shows.
- **Media**: listing videos and 3D tours.
- **Reviews**: show or hide your Reviews page.
- **Areas** ("Areas You Serve"): your town pages (see below).
- **Google** ("How You Look on Google"): your name, headline, tab icon and sub-links, with a live Google preview.
- **Domain** ("Your Domain"): your free address, or a domain you own.

### Saving and publishing

- Changes stay private until you publish.
- **Save draft** keeps your work without making it live. The bar shows "Saved - not published yet".
- **Publish** (or **Publish changes**) puts it live. You'll see "Your website is live."
- If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the app asks you to save a draft or publish first.

### Town pages (Areas)

Each town you add gets its own page with real schools, transportation, parks and market data.

1. Open the **Areas** tab.
2. Type a **Town name** and the 2-letter state (for example, Villanova, PA), then add it.
3. New towns show **Researching...** for a short while.

Tips:

- You can add up to 15 cities, towns, neighborhoods or market areas.
- Use the arrows to put your main town first. Your website lists towns in that order.
- Removing a town asks "Stop serving this area?". Its page will no longer appear on your site.
- Every plan can set "Towns you serve" in Account setup. Only IDX turns them into website pages.

### Pop-up forms

Every lead button on your website opens one of your real forms in a window over the page, so visitors never leave your site. Examples are "What's my home worth?", "Start my home search" and a home's tour request. Every answer lands in your Leads. To change the forms themselves, go to **Tools → Forms & Invites**.

### Blog on your site

Your published blog posts appear on your site's Blog page. Add the **Latest Articles** section to show your newest posts on the home page. Write posts in **Tools → Blogs**.

### Turning the website on or off

1. Go to **Settings → IDX Website**.
2. Use the **IDX Website** switch:
   - **On**: "your IDX website is live on your addresses".
   - **Off**: "you're using a website of your own". The app asks **Turn off your IDX website?**.

When it's off:

- **Nothing is deleted.** Every new QR code, Guest Pass, Showcase, form and copied link uses openhousepal.com instead.
- Your Agent Hub, open houses and leads keep working.
- Switch it back on any time and it's back exactly as published.

The same Settings tab also shows:

- **Your Website**: whether it's **Published & live**, **Draft — not published yet** or **Turned off — not visible to anyone**, plus its address and a copy button.
- **View My Website** and **Edit My Website**.
- **IDX Domain**.
- **More website settings**: quick links to Pages & sections, Look & colors, Featured homes, Towns you serve, Google and Domain.

### Your web address and your own domain

- Every IDX website gets a free address, **yourname.openhousepal.com**, set up automatically.
- In the **Domain** tab you can:
  - Choose **I already have one** to connect a domain you own.
  - Choose **Get a new one** to buy one inside OpenHousePal. It's registered in your name, with your home address kept private, and set up for you. You'll see the price per year before you pay.
- Some agents may see "Connecting your own domain opens soon" or "Buying a domain here opens soon". That means the option isn't switched on for your account yet. Your free address works in the meantime.
- Once your own domain is connected and live, the **Use my own domain for my links** switch turns on. New sign-in QR codes, Guest Passes, Showcases, forms and copied links then use your domain.
- Links you already handed out keep working.

### Common questions

- **I published but visitors don't see it.** Check **Settings → IDX Website**. If it says **Turned off**, switch it on.
- **Will turning the site off break my printed QR codes?** No. Your sign-in codes, forms and Showcases stay on openhousepal.com, and nothing is deleted.

**Where:** the gear icon (on a phone: **Account → Settings**). The title is "Your account, your way". The tabs are **Profile**, **Notifications**, **Tools**, **IDX Website** (IDX plan only), **Billing** and **Account**. Changes save on their own. A small bar at the top shows when they've saved.

### Profile tab

- **Profile photo**: tap **Upload photo** or **Change photo**. Drag the circle to reposition it.
- **Full Name**, **Title**, **Brokerage** (all required), and **Team**.
- **About**: up to 280 characters, kept to 3 lines so your Brag Sheet fits on one page.

- **The middle of your QR codes**: choose **Our house logo**. On IDX you can also choose **Your logo** or **Your initials**. Codes you already printed keep working.

- Showcase activity
- Daily briefing
- Spotlight weekly report (Mondays)
- Liked homes (hourly)
- Open house host requests
- Team & broadcasts

Some emails can't be turned off: sign-in codes and password resets, billing and plan notices, and support replies and connection problems.

### Tools tab

A quick check of **Calendar**, **Reviews** and **CRM**. Each row shows whether it's set up, with a **Manage** or **Open & Set Up** button that opens that tool's page.

Go to **Tools → Email** → **Send from your own email** to connect Gmail. Your client emails then come from your real address, land in the inbox and show in your Sent folder. If your email ever can't send, OpenHousePal sends it instead, so nothing is lost. Outlook shows **Coming soon**. You can also connect your email during Account setup ("Your email" step).

See "Plans and billing" above.

  - To change your sign-in email, MLS ID or license number, tap **Contact support**.
  - Shortcuts: **Your team →**, **Agent Hub →** (on IDX: **Website & domain →**) and **Billing →**.

### 6. Help and support tickets

**Where:** the question-mark icon (on a phone: **Account → Help**). The page is titled "We're here to help". Tickets are split into **Open** and **Closed** tabs.

**To open a ticket:**

1. Tap **Submit a Ticket**.
2. Pick what it's about:
   - **Contact support**: a question. You'll be asked "What do you need help with?" and "Tell us more", plus **How urgent is it?**.
   - **Report a bug**: you'll be asked to "Sum it up in a few words", "What happened?", "What did you expect to happen?" and **Where did it happen?**.
   - **Suggest a feature**: you'll be asked "Your idea in a few words", "What would you like?" and "How would it help you?".
3. Add up to a few **Screenshots** if you like.
4. Send it. The button reads **Send to support**, **Send bug report** or **Send my idea**. You'll get a reference number.

A real person replies right on this page, and you get a notification when they do. Type in **Type a reply...** to answer. Use **Close Ticket** or **Reopen** as needed.

### 7. Account setup (the first-time walkthrough)

**What it is:** a simple, step-by-step setup. Everything saves as you type, and it remembers where you left off.

**Where:** the clipboard icon (on a phone: **Account → Account setup**). Reopen it any time.

### The steps

The steps are grouped. Which ones you see depends on your plan:

- **About you**: About you, How to reach you, License number, Your photo, Your story, Your socials. The first three are **Required**.
- **Your pages**: Your sales, Reviews, Your brand, Agent Hub, Your forms, Email signature.
- **Your open houses**: Open house defaults.
- **Staying in touch**: Alerts & follow-ups, Calendar, Your email, Your CRM.
- **Your website** (called **Your towns** on plans other than IDX): Towns you serve, Website homes, Your website, Web address. The last three are IDX only.

Plan differences:

- Agent Hub steps (Your story, Your socials, Agent Hub, Your forms) appear on Premium, IDX and No-MLS Premium.
- Reviews appears on Premium, IDX and No-MLS Premium.
- Email signature appears on IDX and No-MLS Premium.

### Moving through

- Use **Continue**, **Back** and **Skip**. Each optional step tells you what happens if you skip it.
- The last step's button says **Finish**.
- For Calendar, Your email and Your CRM, you can say you don't use them:
  - **I don't need a calendar**
  - **Keep sending from OpenHousePal**
  - **I don't use a CRM**
  - That removes the step from your to-do list. You can undo it later.
- The step list marks steps as **Skipped**, **Not using**, **Required** or **Done**. After an upgrade, new steps are marked **New for your plan**.
- Waiting on your broker? You can do the "About you" steps early. Everything is ready the moment you're approved.

When you finish, you'll see shortcuts such as **Open my Agent Hub**, **My email signature** and **My website**, depending on your plan.


## Open Houses, Dashboard and Activity - How-To Guide

Plans referred to below: **No-MLS Basic**, **No-MLS Premium**, **Basic**, **Premium**, **IDX**. "MLS plans" means Basic, Premium and IDX. "No-MLS plans" means No-MLS Basic and No-MLS Premium.

### Where things live in the menu

- **Dash** (top menu on a computer; "Dashboard" in the phone menu) - your daily home page.
- **Opens** (top menu; "Open Houses" in the phone menu). Hover it (or open it on a phone) to see:
  - **My Opens** - your open house kits and sign-ins.
  - **Discovery** - find other agents' listings you can host. MLS plans only.
  - **Manager** - your own listings: hosts, requests and insights. MLS plans only.
- **Leads > Activity** - the full history of everything that happened on your account.

A red number on **Opens** means something is waiting on you (a host request, a reply, or a guest request).

---

### 1. Dash (Dashboard)

**What it is:** one screen showing what needs your attention today - new leads, recent activity, open houses and follow-ups. Every plan has it.

**What you'll see:**
- **Header counts:** **Unread** (new leads), **Open houses** (host requests or replies waiting on you) and **Follow-ups** (due now). It updates on its own.
- **Quick-create buttons:** **Create Open House Kit**, **Create Showcase**, **Create Form**, **Create Design**, **Create Blog Post**, **Create CMA**, **Media Lab**. If your plan doesn't include a tool, its button shows a lock (tap it to see the upgrade) or is hidden. On a phone these buttons aren't shown on the Dash - use the menu.
- **Today's Rundown** - your daily briefing. Tap it to open it right on the page.
- **Account setup card** - the remaining setup steps for your plan (**Start setup**, **Continue setup** or **Pick up where I left off**). Skipped steps stay as small chips you can tap later.
- **Stat tiles** (computer and tablet): **Agent Hub Visitors**, **New Leads**, **Sign-ins**, **Forms Viewed**, **Forms Filled**, **Blog Reads**. Tiles for tools your plan doesn't have are hidden. Drag a tile to reorder it.
- **Yellow banner** "*N* host request(s) waiting on your response" - tap it to open your Host Requests in Manager.
- **Panels:** **Leads**, **Activity**, **Open House Manager** (only if you have listings), **Open House Discovery** (MLS plans), and **Follow-ups**. On a computer you can drag panels to rearrange them.
  - **Follow-ups** jumps to the top when something is due and moves to the bottom when you're caught up. **Hide** / **Show** collapses it for this visit only.
  - **Activity** links to **Full history**; **Leads** links to **View all leads**; Follow-ups links to **All follow-ups**.
  - **Open House Discovery** shows nearby listings that need a host ("Near you to host", closest first) and your own requests ("Waiting on listing agent", "New time offered - reply"). If you haven't set up a search yet it says **Set up your search**.
  - **Open House Manager** shows your listings and who's hosting when ("Hosts needed", "Host booked", "Kit not built").

**Common questions**
- *Why don't I see the Open House Manager panel?* It only appears when you have active listings of your own.
- *Why don't I see Discovery?* Discovery needs an MLS plan (Basic, Premium or IDX).
- *I hid Follow-ups - how do I get it back?* Tap **Show**, or just leave and come back to the Dash.

### 2. My Opens (Open House Kits)

**What it is:** where you make an **Open House Kit** for any home and manage every kit you've made. A kit is reusable - one kit per home, used for every open house you hold there, so you never reprint.

**Where:** Opens > **My Opens**.

### Make a kit - MLS plans
1. Type the address in **Enter property address...** and pick the home from the suggestions.
2. Tap **Generate Kit**.
3. **Similar Homes** step: choose homes similar to this one for a printed Similar Homes sheet (and the guests' Guest Pass). Use **Refine Your Search** (price, **Min Beds**, **Min Baths**, **Min Acres**, search radius) and tap homes to add them. If you're the listing agent, this starts off so guests stay focused on your listing - tap **Pick similar homes instead** if you want it anyway. Tap **Continue**.
4. **Cover photo** step: "Select Cover Image for Sign-in Sheet". Drag the photo in the preview to frame it.
5. **Cheat Sheet** step: "Select Photos for the Open House Cheat Sheet". Drag inside each frame to center it. Tap **Continue**.
6. **Review your kit** ("Your kit"): each document with its settings and a live preview:
   - Sign-In Flyer: **Brand** (your photo, name and brokerage across the photo) and **Ivory / Midnight** (the look of the on-screen sign-in guests see).
   - Similar Homes: **Match** (% Match badges) and **Include** (leave it out or put it back).
   - Brag Sheet: **Sold Stats**.
   - Cheat Sheet: **Recap** (replaces the long MLS description with a short AI "Highlights of..." paragraph).
   Tap **Looks good - next**.
7. **After a guest signs in** ("Guests"): set **Guest Pass**, **Guest Showcases** and **Follow-ups** (see "Switches" below). Tap **Save & Finish**.

### Make a kit - No-MLS plans
1. Type the street address and tap **Generate Kit** ("No Bright MLS needed").
2. **Home details**: street address, city, state, ZIP, price, square feet, bedrooms, bathrooms, and your photos (JPG or PNG; tap **Make cover** on the one you want first).
3. Choose the cover photo, review the kit, set what happens after sign-in, and tap **Save & Finish**.
No-MLS kits have no Similar Homes sheet and no Cheat Sheet (both are built from MLS data).

### Your Opens - the kit cards
Each kit appears under **Your Opens** with a search box (**Search your opens...**) and totals for **Guests**, **Completion** and **Unrepresented**. Each card has:
- **Visitors** - everyone who signed in (see "Visitors" below).
- **Kit** - opens **Documents**: the printable pieces.
- **Kiosk** - turns a tablet into a sign-in station (see "Kiosk").
- **Notes** - your private note for this open house. On an open you're hosting for another agent it says **Notes & Info** and also shows the listing agent's host notes.
- Trash icon - remove the kit.
- Switches across the top (see "Switches").

### Documents (the Kit button)
Tap a document to open its print preview.
- **Sign In** (Branded QR Sign in Sheet): **Brand**, **Ivory/Midnight**, **Clipboard** (a printable pencil-and-paper sign-in sheet with the same questions as the QR sign-in) and **Refresh** (pulls the latest price onto the flyer).
- **Similar Homes** (MLS plans): **Choose Homes** / **Edit**, **Match %**, **On / Off**, and **Refresh** (updates prices and removes homes that sold or are no longer active).
- **Brag Sheet**: your bio and recent sales (plus a Showcase pitch page on Premium and IDX when Showcases and Similar Homes are on). **Sold Stats** and **Resync Sales** (pulls your latest profile and recent sales).
- **Cheat Sheet** (MLS plans, MLS-linked home): facts, description and what's nearby. **Edit** (photos), **Recap**, **Refresh Data** (re-pulls price, beds/baths/sqft and description).

### Switches on each open house
What happens after a guest signs in. Each switch only shows if your plan has it. Changes save right away.
- **Guest Showcases** (Premium, IDX): ON - every guest without an agent gets their own Showcase of similar homes, plus a Showcase button on their Guest Pass. Starts OFF on your own listing. When you switch it off, a window **Turn off Showcases?** asks what guests still get: **Similar homes** on their Guest Pass, and/or **Showcases** (a "Get my Showcase" button - one is made only if they tap it). Choose **Turn off Showcases** or **Keep them on**.
- **Guest Pass** (Basic, Premium, IDX): ON - right after signing in, the guest's phone opens their Guest Pass: this home, your Similar Homes and Cheat Sheet as web pages, one-tap seller disclosure and second-tour requests, their Showcase (if on), and your Spotlight (if you have one for this listing). OFF - guests just see the thank-you screen. Never shown on a Kiosk tablet.
- **Similar Homes** (MLS plans): turned on/off on the Documents screen (**On / Off**) or in the kit. Off means no Similar Homes sheet and no other homes on the Guest Pass.
- **Opens Hub / Opens Nothing** (No-MLS Premium): after sign-in, the guest's phone opens your Agent Hub, or just the thank-you screen. The Hub option needs your Agent Hub set up first.
- **+ Spotlight / Finish Spotlight** (IDX, your own listing): make a Listing Spotlight for this home; once published it's featured at the top of every Guest Pass.
- **Follow-ups** (set while making the kit): **On** - a "reach out" reminder for each guest without an agent, with a ready-to-send text. **Off** - no follow-ups from this open house (guests still land in Leads). If your calendar is connected, **Put them on my calendar** controls whether they also go on your calendar.

### Visitors
Tap **Visitors** on a card. You'll see **Visitors**, **No agent** and **Has agent** counts, then everyone grouped by day with email and phone (tap to copy, email or call).
- **Star** a visitor to star their lead. If they have a Showcase you're asked whether to **Star Showcase too** or **Just the lead**.
- **Showcase** switch (Premium, IDX; guests without an agent): turn on to give this one guest a Showcase - their invite email goes out after the delay you set in Studio > Email. Turn off to cancel a waiting invite and turn their Showcase off.
- **Notes** - a note on this visitor, shared with their lead profile.
- **Activity** - everything they did on their Guest Pass (only shows once they've used it).

### Disclosure & Second Visits
MLS plans. When a guest asks for the seller's disclosure or a second visit (from the Cheat Sheet QR or their Guest Pass), it shows on My Opens - at the very top while it's waiting on you. Tabs **Upcoming** and **Past**. When you've handled one, tap **Mark sent** (disclosure) or **Mark scheduled** (second visit); it moves to Past and its follow-up is checked off.

### You're confirmed to host
MLS plans. Opens another agent approved you to host appear here. Tap **Open kit** if you already have a kit for that home ("Kit ready - use the same one"), or **Build kit** to make one.

### Kiosk
1. Tap **Kiosk** on a card.
2. **Choose Sign-In Mode**:
   - **Sign-In Kiosk** - guests fill out the form on the tablet; it resets after each guest.
   - **Sign-In Sheet** - no printer needed: rotating property photos plus a QR code guests scan with their own phone.
3. To leave Kiosk mode, press and hold the exit button.

### What guests see when they sign in
**Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, then "Do you have an active agreement with a real estate agent?" - tapping **No** or **Yes** signs them in. Everyone lands in your Leads.

**Common questions**
- *Why don't I see Similar Homes or the Cheat Sheet?* They need an MLS plan and a home picked from the MLS search. Typed-in homes don't have them.
- *Why don't I see Guest Pass?* Guest Pass is on Basic, Premium and IDX only.
- *Why don't I see Guest Showcases?* Showcases are on Premium and IDX only. (On Basic, a "You're missing out right now" banner may show guests who would have gotten one.)
- *How do I remove a kit?* Tap the trash icon, then **Remove Listing**. Visitors, leads and notes are kept, and QR codes keep working - but the card and its PDFs leave My Opens. There's no undo button; make a new kit if you need one again.
- *Does Ivory/Midnight change my printout?* No - only the on-screen sign-in page.
- *Do I need a new kit for each open house?* No. One kit per home is reused every time.

**Where:** Opens > **Discovery** (or **Find Open Houses** on My Opens).

**Set your search (saved automatically):**
- **Enter address or landmark** and pick a distance in **miles**.
- Filters: **Brokerages**, **Exclude Agents / Only Agents**, **Cities**, **Districts**, **Min Bedrooms**, **Min Bathrooms**, **Min Sqft**, **Min Price**. **Reset All** clears them.

**Request to host a home:**

4. **Say hello and send** - a note is written for you; change it if you like. Tap **Send request** (or **Send 2 requests**).

If the listing agent isn't on OpenHousePal, you get an **Example Outreach Email** to copy and send yourself.

On a request you can **Accept New Time**, **Decline**, propose **Other time**, or **Withdraw request** / **Can't host anymore?**.

**Common questions**

- *It says "Coming Soon · Hosting once it's active".* The MLS doesn't allow showings until it goes active.

## OpenHousePal How-To: Leads, Follow-ups, Email Tracker, Showcases, CMA and Reviews

Plan names used below are the ones on the pricing cards: **No-MLS Basic**, **No-MLS Premium**, **Basic**, **Premium**, **IDX**.

Where things live in the top menu:
- **Leads** menu: **Leads**, **Email Tracker**, **Follow-ups**, **Activity** (plus **Action Plans**, marked "Soon" - not available yet).
- **Showcases** is its own item in the top menu.
- **Tools** menu: **CMA** and **Reviews** (among other tools).

---

### 1. Leads

**What it is:** One list of everyone who signed in at an open house, filled out a form, asked about a home or used their Showcase - one lead per person, with everything they've done.

**Where:** Top menu **Leads** > **Leads**. Page heading: "Every lead, one place".

**Plans:** Every plan.

### The Leads list
- **Tabs across the top:** **Needs you**, **No agent**, **New**, **Contacted**, **In talks**, **Signed**, **All**, **Has agent**, **Hidden**. Each tab shows a count.
  - **Needs you** = people with something waiting on you today: a follow-up that's due or overdue, or a request (book a showing, send the disclosure, reply to a comment, update their Showcase search).
  - **No agent** = everyone without an agent, plus anyone with an agent you left a note on.
- **Search box:** "Search name, email, phone or open house".
- **Filters:** **Tags** (only tags you've added inside a lead show here), **Came in from** (Open houses, Showcases, Forms, Spotlights & listings, Website & blog, Zillow & portals, Reviews, Other). Picking **Open houses** adds a second menu to pick **which open house**.
- **Order:** Action needed first, Warmest first, Latest activity, Newest leads, Name A-Z, Opens my emails most, Opens my emails least.
- **Export:** downloads the leads you're looking at as a spreadsheet (CSV).
- **Header numbers:** "Need you" and "New" (new leads in the period you pick: This week, Last 30 days, Last 3 months, This year, All time).
- **Shortcuts at the top:** **Follow-ups**, **Email Tracker**, and a CRM chip.

### Warmth (the Hot / Warm / Cool score)
Every action earns points. Asking to see a home counts most, then questions, sign-ins and forms, then likes and comments. Views and email opens count a little. Recent activity counts most - after 30 quiet days the points are gone. **Hot** is 40+, **Warm** 15+, below that **Cool**. Scrolling homes or opening emails alone can't make someone Hot, and your own previews never count. Tap **How warmth works** on the page for the same explanation.

### Quick actions on each row
- **Call**, **Text**, **Copy email**.
- **Star** (gold star) - stars the lead *and* their Showcase together.
- **Mark that you reached out** button - stamps "last contacted" and moves a New lead to Contacted.
- **"..." (More)** menu: **Move to** New / Contacted / In talks / Signed, **Mark contacted**, **Mark as seen**, **Hide** (or **Show again** on a hidden lead), **Delete**.
- **Mark all seen** (appears when leads have red "new" dots): clears every red dot at once. Follow-ups and requests stay in "Needs you". This can't be undone.

### Inside one lead
Click a lead to open it. You'll see:
- **Edit** (next to their name): change name, email or phone, then **Save**. If you change the email and they have a Showcase, you're asked **"Send a new invite?"** - choose **No thanks** or **Yes, send one**, then pick **Showcase from an open house** or **Normal Showcase invite**. Their Showcase now goes to the new address either way.
- **Call / Text / Email** buttons and copy buttons for phone and email.
- **Where they stand** menu: New ("Haven't talked yet"), Contacted, In talks ("Actively working together"), Signed ("Your client").
- **Do they have an agent?** menu: No agent, Not sure, Has an agent (moves them to the "Has agent" tab).
- **How they found you:** Came from, First touch, Latest, and "What they told you". **See it in Activity** jumps to the full entry.
- **Waiting on you:** each open request with **Open** and **Done** (Done = "I've handled this - clear it").
- **Follow-ups** section (see section 2), with **Alert settings** and **All follow-ups**.
- **Their Showcase** card: Homes / Liked / Passed counts, last active, whether they get Showcase emails, **Open** and **Their link** (copies their Showcase link).
- **Emails** card: what they were sent, opened, clicked, and whether automatic emails are on, weekly or off.
- **Activity:** everything they've done, filter by **Everything**, **Forms & requests**, **Showcase**.
- **Open houses they came to.**
- **Notes:** type in "Add a note..." and add it. Notes also show on their open house sign-in and on their follow-ups. You can edit or delete your notes.
- **Tags:** type in "Add a tag..." and press the + button; click the x on a tag to remove it.
- **Record:** Lead since, Last contacted, and a **Mark contacted** / **Contacted them again** button. Checking off a follow-up marks them contacted for you.
- **Hide** ("Take them off your lists - nothing is deleted") and **Delete**.

### Per-lead alert switches
In a lead's Follow-ups section tap **Alert settings** ("Just for [name]"). Only this person changes - other leads aren't affected.
- **Put [name]'s follow-ups on my calendar** - on: each follow-up shows on your calendar with an alert. Turning it off asks **"Still want [name]'s follow-ups in OpenHousePal?"**: **Yes, keep them here** (they stay on the Follow-ups page and Dash, just not your calendar) or **No, stop them** (no new follow-ups for this person).
- **Email me when [name] likes or comments** - off means you'll still see it in their activity, you just won't get the email.

The same switches also appear on the "Mark as done" page that opens from a calendar alert.

### Common questions
- **How do I add a lead by hand?** There is no separate "add lead" button on the Leads page. People become leads when they sign in, fill out a form, or use a Showcase. On Premium and IDX you can create a Showcase for someone (Showcases > **Create**) - that adds them to your Leads too.
- **Hide vs Delete?** **Hide** takes them off your lists; nothing is deleted and you can bring them back from the **Hidden** tab with **Show again**. **Delete** removes them for good: their activity and notes, your Activity feed, the Email Tracker - and **their Showcase is deleted too**. Automatic emails to them stop. Delete can't be undone.
- **Why is someone not in "Needs you"?** Only things due today/overdue or unhandled requests put them there. A follow-up set for a later day is planned, not waiting.
- **Why don't I see follow-up chips on my leads?** Follow-ups may be turned off (see the Follow-ups switch below).
- **"That email already belongs to another lead."** Two leads can't share one email - open the other lead instead.

### 2. Follow-ups

**What it is:** Reminders of who to reach out to and when. Most are added for you automatically; you can add your own.

**Where:** Top menu **Leads** > **Follow-ups** (heading "Never miss a follow-up"), or the **Follow-ups** button on the Leads page, or inside any lead.

**Plans:** Every plan. Some automatic situations only appear on plans that have the matching feature (Showcase situations on Premium and IDX; Website sign-in on IDX; Unhappy review on Premium, IDX and No-MLS Premium).

### Turn follow-ups on or off (master switch)
The **Follow-ups** switch at the top of the page. Off = "No automatic follow-ups"; nothing new is added and follow-ups are hidden from your Dashboard, Leads page and daily email. Your situations, messages and phone alerts are saved - flip it back on to pick up where you left off. Useful if your CRM already reminds you.

### Add your own follow-up
1. Tap **Add** (on the Follow-ups page) or **Add a follow-up for [name]** (inside a lead).
2. Type what needs doing ("What needs doing?").
3. Under **When**, pick **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Pick a day…** or **Someday**. Quick picks also include **In 3 days** and **Next Monday**.
4. Optional: **Add a time** - with a time it buzzes your phone; with no time it's an all-day item.
5. Tap **Add follow-up**.

### Working a follow-up card
- **Mark done** (and **Undo - mark not done**). Checking one off also marks the person contacted.
- **"..." (Snooze or delete)** > **Snooze**: **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days**, **Next week** (it keeps its time of day), or delete it.
- Ready-to-send message: **Text**, **Copy email**, or copy the message. If the message has a blank like [ADD DATE & TIME], replace it before sending.
- Showing requests show **Book in ShowingTime** with the listing agent's phone, email and MLS#, and **Mark scheduled** once booked.
- **Suggested from what they just did:** a suggested follow-up you can add with **Add for tomorrow**.
- **Inbox** button: every follow-up added in the last two weeks, newest first.

### Automatic follow-ups (the situations)
Further down the Follow-ups page, under **Automatic follow-ups**, grouped as **Open houses**, **Form responses** and **Showcases, listings & reviews**. Each situation has:
- An on/off switch ("Off - nothing is added for this situation").
- **When:** **Soon**, **In 2 hrs**, or **Next day**.
- **Style:** **Set time** (buzzes your phone) or **All day** (doesn't buzz). Both show as Free on your calendar.
- **Message** (or **Alert** for alert-only situations): open it to edit the ready-to-send text. Some situations are **Alert only** - there's nothing to edit.

Situations: Open house sign-in, Similar Homes QR scan, Second visit request, Disclosure request, Setup My Own Showcase, I'm Buying form, How Can We Help form (separate buyer and seller messages), Any other form, Invite RSVP, Showing request, Showcase comment, Showcase likes, Showcase search change, Listing inquiry, Website sign-in, Unhappy review (1-3 stars).

**How follow-ups show on your calendar:** choose how many minutes before a set-time follow-up your phone buzzes, and whether it also buzzes at the start. The page shows **Connect your calendar** / **Reconnect your calendar** if your calendar isn't syncing - follow-ups only reach your phone once a calendar is connected.

### From the calendar alert (on your phone)
Every follow-up's calendar event has links:
- **Mark as done** - checks it off in OpenHousePal and crosses it out on your calendar. Below it, "Did you also do these?" lists your other follow-ups due today. **Undo - not done yet** reverses it.
- **Text [name] from your phone** - opens a page where one tap on **Text** opens your own Messages app with their number and the suggested text already written (it goes from your real cell number). **Email** opens your mail app with it written as an email. Either one checks the follow-up off. On a computer you get the text with **Copy text** and a QR code - **Scan with your phone**.
- **Save to contacts** - adds them to your phone, notes included.
- Missed follow-ups move to today each morning for up to a week. There's only ever one open follow-up per person - no duplicates.

### 3. Email Tracker

**What it is:** Every email your clients were sent, whether they opened it and whether they clicked. People who stop opening get fewer emails, so the rest keep landing in the inbox.

**Where:** Top menu **Leads** > **Email Tracker** (heading "Every email, tracked").

**Plans:** Every plan.

- Numbers at the top (last 30 days): **Sent**, **Opened**, **Clicked**, **Slowed** (people on weekly or no automatic emails).
- Tabs: **People** and **Sent emails**.
- People filters: **All**, **On**, **Weekly**, **Off**, **Not in your leads**. Sort: Latest email, Most engaged, Least engaged, Name A-Z.
- Each person: **Turn off** / **Turn back on** their automatic emails, and **Their emails** to see what they were sent. A **Why:** line explains a slowdown.

**The automatic rules:**
- **Emails on** - normal.
- **Weekly only** - they left emails unopened on 3 different days; daily Showcase updates become one weekly email. Back to daily as soon as they open one.
- **Emails off** - they also ignored 3 weekly emails; automatic emails stop. They restart by themselves when the person opens an email or uses their Showcase.
- Personal emails (tour confirmation, reply to their comment, the thank-you after signing in) always send.
- **Unsubscribed**, bounced ("Fix their email address to send again") or marked-as-spam people can't be switched back on by you.

### 4. Showcases

**What it is:** A personal home search for a buyer. They like, pass, comment and request tours; you get alerts, and they get new-match and price-drop emails. Guests without an agent who sign in at your open houses get one automatically.

**Where:** Top menu **Showcases** (heading "Homes picked for every buyer").

**Plans:** Premium and IDX. On Basic it shows locked with an upgrade prompt. The No-MLS plans don't have Showcases.

### The Showcases page
- Search: "Search visitor, property, location...".
- **Tours** button: tour requests waiting on you from every Showcase. **Activity** button: likes, comments, tour requests and search changes from your clients.
- **Budgets** filter and sort: **Activity**, **New matches**, **Newest**, **Fell asleep** (Inactive tab only), **Came from**.
- **Active** / **Inactive** tabs, and a **New matches** filter.
- Each Showcase card: star, client email/phone copy, new-match count (**Dismiss new matches**), activity badges, **Edit**, **Share**, **Delete**, and an **On / Off** switch.

### Create a Showcase for a client
1. Tap **Create** (or **+ Create a Showcase** when you have none).
2. **About them:** Client's full name, Email, Phone (optional), Showcase name (filled in from the client's name - change it to anything). Switch **Email [name] their Showcase** on to send them their link, what it looks for and a few homes as soon as you create it.
3. Tap **Continue**, then answer **Why is [name] looking?** (Looking to buy soon, Just browsing, Interested in area, Investment opportunity, Curious about property) and **Does [name] have an agent?**
4. Tap **Set the search area** and set the search on the map: price, Bedrooms & baths, Size, lot & age, **Towns**, **School districts**, **Counties**, a **Search center** with a radius, or **Draw a search area**; Property type; **Match score** ("Showing the % Match badge"); and a **Comparing to** home.
5. Tap **Create Showcase**. The client is added to your Leads.

### Open one Showcase
Click a card. You'll see the client's name, email and phone, and:
- Tabs: **Matches**, **Liked**, **Disliked**, **Sold/Removed**. Sort by Best Match, Days on Market, Last Updated, Price per Sq Ft, Square Feet, Lot Size, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Year Built.
- **Comparing to** / **Searching around** bar - tap to change the compare home or the radius.
- **Share** (copies the link; if the link isn't on yet it asks you to turn it on), **Edit**, **Tours**, **Turn Off / Turn On**.
- **Emails** switches: **To [client]** (their update emails) and **To me** (your emails about this Showcase). On a computer these show as **Visitor** and **Agent**.
- **Select** mode: pick homes, then **Recommend**, **Love**, **Pass**, **To Sold/Removed** (off-market homes only), **Remove**, or **Send** (opens Messages or your email app with the home links; you can save your own intro message for every Showcase).

### Change the search
Tap **Edit** (card or Showcase), change anything, then **Save search**. Their client hears about it in the afternoon email: what the search is now and homes added or gone.
If the client asked you to change it, you'll see "[name] asked you to refine their search" with their message - tap **Edit Search**, or the x to dismiss it.

### Tours
Tap **Tours**. For each request: **Confirm**, then pick the time and **Yes, Confirm Tour** - only do this once it's scheduled in ShowingTime and the listing agent accepted, because it tells the client their tour is happening (they get a confirmation email and it's added to your connected calendar). **Unconfirm** reverses it. **Mark Done** ("You've answered this another way - stop flagging it") clears a request without telling the client it's booked; **Reopen** undoes that.

### Star, sleep and wake
- **Star** a Showcase (or star the lead - they're linked): it sorts first and never falls asleep. Starring an asleep Showcase wakes it and moves it to Active.
- **Asleep (Inactive):** if a client ignores 3 weekly emails in a row and you haven't touched them in 2 weeks (opened the Showcase or lead, marked contacted, wrote a note, checked off a follow-up), their Showcase goes to sleep: no new matches, no emails, no alerts. Nothing is deleted and the link still works. A client who keeps using their Showcase stays awake even if they ignore emails.
- It **wakes by itself** when the client opens their Showcase or an old email, and catches up on homes that came on the market while it slept. You can wake it with **Turn On** / the **On** switch, or by starring it.
- **Turn Off** ("Put to sleep: no new matches, no emails") does the same by hand.

### Add one home to a client's Showcase
On a home's page, use **Add to a Showcase** and pick the client. It's marked as your pick (Recommended), stays even if it doesn't match their search, and counts as new for them.

### What the buyer sees
Their own Showcase link with **Matches / Liked / Disliked / Sold/Removed**, a heart to like, pass, comments, tour requests, **Change your search** (they can change it themselves or send you a message), an **Emails** switch for update emails, and "Your Upcoming Tours" once you confirm. When you open their link yourself you'll see "Your clicks here don't count as theirs."

### Showcases and open houses
Each open house has its own Showcases switch on the Open Houses page. Turning it off asks **Turn off Showcases?** and lets you choose what the guest's Guest Pass still offers: **Similar homes** and/or a **"Get my Showcase"** button (one is made only if they tap it).

### Common questions
- **How do I undo deleting a Showcase?** You can't. **Delete Showcase** permanently removes its homes, likes, comments and search; the client stays in your Leads. It can also remove open follow-ups about that Showcase (the window lists them). To pause instead, use **Turn Off**.
- **Why don't I see Showcases?** It's Premium and IDX only. If you see a broker authorization message, finish **Broker Authorization** from the top menu.
- **Why did a Showcase move to Inactive?** It fell asleep (see above) or someone turned it off. Star it or turn it on.

### 5. CMA (Comparative Market Analysis)

**What it is:** A client-ready pricing report from comparable sales plus your own judgment, with an AI analysis that reads every comp's photos and data, rates each comp and proposes a value.

**Where:** Top menu **Tools** > **CMA** (heading "Price it right, prove it").

**Plans:** Basic, Premium and IDX. Not on the No-MLS plans. Broker authorization is required.

### Your CMA list
Search ("Search by address or client..."), tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Ready**, **Shared**. Each CMA card shows the Fair Value and has **Copy Share Link**, **Download PDF** / **Save the PDF**, a theme switch (light or **Midnight**), and **Delete** (permanent).

### Build a CMA
1. Tap **Create New CMA**.
2. Optional **Client name**, then choose **This CMA is for a...**: **Seller / Listing** ("What should this home list for?") or **Buyer / Offer** ("What should my buyer offer?"). Tap **Continue to Subject Property**.
3. **Subject** - "Select the Subject Property": **Search Address**, **My Listings**, or **Enter Manually** (fill in the home's facts - the more complete, the better the value). Fix any fact that isn't right, then **Find comps**.
4. **Comps** - top matches are auto-selected; add or remove as you see fit. Use **Refine Your Search** (Status, Sold in the last, Radius, Min Beds/Baths/Acres, School District) if you need more. Then continue.
5. **Analysis** - tap **Create AI Analysis**. It shows how much of your monthly AI allowance it uses. On any comp you can write your own note or **Mark Irrelevant** with a reason.
6. **Pricing** - your pricing analysis (Seller CMAs include a recommended list price and listing strategy/prep notes; Buyer CMAs include an offer suggestion).
7. **Review** - **Presentation**: hide any section you don't want the client to see (Subject Property, Comparable Overview, Location Context, Individual Comparables, Market Context, Market Analysis, Current Competition, Agent Final Analysis). **Complete & Send**: **Mark Ready**, **Download** / **Open PDF**, **Share link**, **My Signature** (adds your signature card at the end of the web version), **Visitor Theme** (Ivory or Midnight).

### Common questions
- **Do I have to re-share after editing?** No - the share link always shows the latest saved version.
- **Why don't I see CMA?** It needs an MLS plan (Basic, Premium or IDX).
- **The AI analysis said I'm out of allowance.** It uses your monthly AI allowance; the meter on the Analysis step shows where you are.

### 2. Listing Spotlight (a website for one of your listings)

**What it is:** a cinematic standalone website for one of your current listings - photos, the story, the town, open houses, documents and a contact form. AI writes the first draft for you. **IDX plan only.**

1. Go to **Tools > Listing Spotlight** and click **New Spotlight page** (or **Create your first site** if you have none). You can also tap a home under **Ready for a Spotlight**.

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1. **Design** ("Pick a Design") | Choose **Luxe** (midnight and gold) or **Editorial Estate** (paper-light, like a magazine). Click **Select**. Same content either way - switch any time. |

Under **Photo beside the story**, AI picks one ("Picked by AI"); tap **Choose a different photo** to change it. |
| 4. **Stand-outs** ("What Stands Out") | **Featured amenities** (up to 12, tap to add/remove), **Highlights** (up to 8 short phrases that scroll across the page; you can ask AI to suggest more), **Notable facts** (optional, up to 3, e.g. "45,000-Gallon Pool"). |

### 10. Blogs (incl. the AI writer)

**What it is:** local-market articles that appear on your Agent Hub and (IDX) your IDX website. **Premium, IDX and No-MLS Premium.**

**Where:** **Tools > Blogs**. Click **Create a Blog** (or **Create**).

### Write one
1. **New Article** - "What's it about?" Type a topic (or pick an idea).
2. "How should we write it?" - **Write it with AI** ("A few quick questions about the area and your take, then a complete article in your voice") or **Write it myself** / **Start from scratch**.
3. AI writer questions: the angle, audience (e.g. First-time buyers, Move-up buyers, Luxury buyers, Relocating, Homeowners), towns, **Key points to cover**, **Numbers you want cited**, your sales or active listings to include, an optional YouTube video, tone (**Warm & personal**, **Professional**, **Refined luxury**, **Energetic**, **Straight talk**) and **Length** (**Quick read**, **Standard**, **In-depth guide**). Then **Write my article**.
4. It researches, writes and fact-checks ("Usually 2-4 minutes... Uses your monthly AI usage."). You can **Keep working - tell me when it's done**; your bell lights up when it's ready. "A try that fails is never charged."
5. It's **Saved as a draft - nothing is published until you say so.**

### Edit and publish
- In the editor: add a cover photo, sections, category, tags, look (Classic, Luxe [IDX and No-MLS Premium], Editorial, Modern, Midnight), and search/share titles. AI section tools: **Make it shorter**, **Expand with more depth**, **Make it more local**, **Make it more personal**, **Rewrite it fresher**.
- **Publish** → **Publish now**, or schedule it (e.g. "Next Monday 8 AM"). A published article can be updated ("Update published article") or unpublished.
- Where it shows: switches **Show on my Agent Hub**, **Featured on Agent Hub**, and **Show on my IDX Site** (IDX). On the Blogs list, each card's **Shows on** has **Agent Hub** and **IDX Site** switches.
- Changing an article's link "breaks links people already shared".

### 11. CRM (send leads out, pull portal leads in)

**What it is:** two separate switches - **Leads OUT** (push every new lead to your CRM) and **Leads IN** (forward Zillow/Realtor.com/portal lead emails to your private address so they appear in Leads tagged "Portal Lead"). **Every plan.**

**Where:** **Tools > CRM**.

### Send leads to your CRM
1. **Which CRM do you use?** - pick yours (Follow Up Boss, Lofty, kvCORE / BoldTrail, Sierra Interactive, Real Geeks, Wise Agent, LionDesk, IXACT Contact, Top Producer, Webhook / Zapier, Another CRM...).
2. **Connect it** - the page shows exactly where to click in your CRM. Follow Up Boss: paste your **Follow Up Boss API key** (best option). Others: paste your CRM's lead-parsing email, or a webhook URL.
3. **Save & send a test lead** - click **Save**, then **Send test lead**, and check your CRM. Turn the sending switch on - "Sending is switched off, so real leads won't go there yet."

### Pull in portal leads
Copy **Your private forwarding address** and follow **How to forward from each portal**. Generating a new address makes the old one stop working immediately.
